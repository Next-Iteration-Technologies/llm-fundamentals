# HR-14 Parental leave

Harbor Labs policy.

Eligible employees receive 16 weeks of maternity leave. Eligible employees
receive 10 working days of paternity leave, to be taken within 60 days of
the child's birth. Eligible employees who adopt a child receive 4 weeks of
adoption leave.

Leave is paid at the employee's base salary. Requests go to People
Operations at least 30 days in advance when the date is known.
