# HR-03 Paid time off

Harbor Labs policy.

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per calendar year,
accrued monthly. A maximum of 5 unused days may be carried into the next
year. Requests of 3 or more consecutive days must be submitted at least
7 days in advance. Sick leave is tracked separately and is not deducted
from the 20-day balance.
