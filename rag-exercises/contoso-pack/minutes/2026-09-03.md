# Minutes 2026-09-03: Northwind onboarding, kick-off

## Attendees

Sam Patel (chair), Emma de Vries, Lee Wong, Noor Haddad, and for Northwind: Anna Visser (office manager) and Daan Mulder (facilities).

## What Northwind needs

Kick-off of the Northwind onboarding. Northwind is an accounting firm with 6 floors in one building in Utrecht and about 300 staff. Northwind wants one cooler on every floor, two on the busy ground floor near the canteen, and still and sparkling water on the management floor. The contract was signed on 2026-09-01 for 36 months.

Anna Visser said the old supplier often ran out of bottles on Mondays. Northwind wants enough bottles for a full week, delivered on a fixed day.

## Success criteria

Success criteria agreed with Northwind: all coolers installed on all 6 floors within 10 working days of the signed contract, extra bottles delivered within 2 working days of a request, and one named contact person at Contoso Water for the first 90 days.

Noor Haddad is the named contact person for the first 90 days. Emma de Vries is the back-up in Utrecht.

## Bottle supply in the Netherlands

Northwind will need about 120 bottles a week. Our current Netherlands supplier BlueRiver delivers about 400 bottles a week to the Utrecht warehouse, and that is already used by existing clients. We need a supplier who can add 150 bottles a week from October.

Candidates: ClearSpring, BlueRiver (more volume), Alpenquell. Lee asks each for a price and a first delivery date.

## Decisions

- Northwind gets 7 coolers: 6 bottle coolers WC-150 and 1 plumbed-in cooler WC-300 for the management floor.
- Weekly delivery day: Tuesday, before 10:00.
