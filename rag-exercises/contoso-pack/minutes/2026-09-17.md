## Site survey results

The site survey at Northwind was done on 2026-09-15 by Lee Wong and Daan Mulder. All 6 floors have a power socket and floor space near the kitchen area. The goods lift can take a trolley with 8 bottles. The loading bay is open from 07:00 to 11:00 on weekdays.

The management floor (floor 6) has a water pipe within 2 metres of the kitchen, so the plumbed-in sparkling cooler WC-300 can be fitted there. No building work is needed.

## ClearSpring: bottles for the Netherlands

ClearSpring presented their offer. ClearSpring is our main supplier in the UK. They are cheaper than BlueRiver per bottle and their bottles are already used by Fabrikam and other London clients. We asked ClearSpring whether they can deliver 150 extra bottles a week to our Utrecht warehouse from October.

Mark Evans will check with their logistics team and come back to us in writing.
