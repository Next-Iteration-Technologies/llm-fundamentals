# POL-RET-01 Archive retention policy

## 1 Purpose

This policy sets how long Contoso Archive Services keeps archived content, and when it is deleted.

## 2 Retention periods

Business documents are kept for 10 years from the end of the year they were archived. Invoices and accounting records are kept for 10 years. HR records are kept for 3 years after the employee leaves.

## 3 Legal hold

A legal hold stops all deletion for the content it covers, whatever its retention period. Only Legal can set or lift a legal hold.

## 4 Deleted mailboxes

Archived mailboxes of deleted accounts are kept for 90 days after deletion, then purged, unless a legal hold applies.

## 5 Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy need written approval from Records management and are reviewed every year.
