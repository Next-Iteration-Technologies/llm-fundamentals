# HR-LEA-NL Annual leave (Netherlands)

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains annual leave for Contoso Water employees who work in the Netherlands. It covers how many days of annual leave Dutch employees get, how to book them, who approves them and when unused days expire.

The policy applies to everyone employed by Contoso Water in the Netherlands, including office staff, warehouse staff and drivers at the Utrecht office. It does not apply to employees in the United Kingdom or Germany. They have their own annual leave policies, HR-LEA-UK and HR-LEA-DE.

The leave year at Contoso Water runs from 1 January to 31 December. Priya Shah, HR Manager, owns this policy. Emma de Vries, HR and office manager in Utrecht, is the local contact for employees in the Netherlands.

## 2 How many days employees in the Netherlands get

Full-time employees in the Netherlands get **25 days of annual leave** each leave year. The 25 days are made up of two parts:

- **20 statutory days.** These are the days that Dutch law requires.
- **5 extra days.** These are given by Contoso Water on top of the law.

Dutch public holidays, such as King's Day and Christmas, come on top of the 25 days. You do not use annual leave for them.

Part-time employees in the Netherlands get the same entitlement in proportion to their hours. For example, someone working 32 hours instead of 40 hours gets 32/40 of 25 days, which is 20 days: 16 statutory days and 4 extra days.

PeopleHub, the HR app, shows both parts of your balance separately, so you can see which days expire first.

## 3 When unused leave expires (Netherlands)

In the Netherlands, the two parts of annual leave expire at different times.

- **Statutory days expire 6 months after the end of the leave year.** Statutory days from 2026 must be used by 1 July 2027. After that date they are gone.
- **Extra days** follow Dutch law for extra days and stay valid for 5 years after the end of the leave year.

When you take annual leave in the Netherlands, PeopleHub always uses your oldest statutory days first. This protects you from losing days.

Example: Pieter, a driver in Utrecht, has 6 days left on 31 December 2026: 4 statutory days and 2 extra days. He must use the 4 statutory days by 1 July 2027. The 2 extra days remain valid for much longer, but his manager will still ask him to plan them.

If you could not take your statutory days because of long sickness, talk to Emma de Vries. Dutch law has special rules for that case.

## 4 How to request annual leave in PeopleHub (Netherlands)

Employees in the Netherlands request all annual leave in PeopleHub, the HR app on your laptop or phone. Do not book leave by mail or by a message to your manager.

1. Open PeopleHub and choose **Time off > Request leave**.
2. Pick the type **Annual leave (NL)**, the first and last day, and half days if needed.
3. Add a short note if it helps, for example "family visit".
4. Press **Submit**. Your manager gets a message straight away.

Give enough notice:

- For 1 or 2 days, ask at least **2 working days** before.
- For 3 days or more, ask at least **2 weeks** before.
- For leave in July or August, ask by **31 March** so routes and cover can be planned.

Do not book travel until your leave shows as **Approved** in PeopleHub.

## 5 Who approves leave in the Netherlands and by when

Your line manager approves annual leave for employees in the Netherlands. For drivers and warehouse staff in Utrecht, this is the Utrecht warehouse team lead, who reports to Lee Wong, Delivery Operations Manager. Managers answer every request within **3 working days**.

Managers look at cover before they approve. In the Utrecht warehouse and delivery team, no more than **2 people** from the same team can be on annual leave on the same day, so that every client still gets their weekly delivery. In autumn 2026 the Northwind onboarding project in Utrecht may also limit leave for the people working on it.

If your request is refused, your manager must give a reason in PeopleHub and suggest other dates. If you think a refusal is unfair, talk to Emma de Vries in Utrecht or Priya Shah at hr@contoso-water.example.

## 6 Holiday allowance and leaving (Netherlands)

Employees in the Netherlands receive a holiday allowance (vakantiegeld) as Dutch law requires. It is paid once a year with the May salary. Your payslip in the PayRight app shows the amount. This is separate from your annual leave days.

Unused annual leave in the Netherlands is not paid out while you work for Contoso Water. If you leave the company, any days you have not taken are paid with your final salary. If you have taken more days than you had earned at that point, the extra days are taken off your final salary.

Leave you have already booked during your notice period stays booked, but please agree it with your manager when you hand in your notice.

## 7 Sickness and public holidays (Netherlands)

If you fall ill during annual leave in the Netherlands, report it to your manager and to the company doctor (bedrijfsarts) on the first day you are ill, the same way as for any sick day. The days you were ill can then be given back to you as annual leave.

If a Dutch public holiday falls on a day you normally work, you get the day off and it does not use annual leave. Part-time employees in the Netherlands get their fair share of public holidays added to their balance.

Your balance, past requests and approved days are always visible in PeopleHub under **Time off**.
