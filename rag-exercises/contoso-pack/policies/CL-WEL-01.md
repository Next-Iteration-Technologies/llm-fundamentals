# CL-WEL-01 Welcome call and the first 90 days

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains how Contoso Water looks after a new client in the first 90 days after the first delivery. It covers the welcome call, the check-in calls at 30 days and 90 days, and what to do when a new client is unhappy.

The first 90 days decide whether a new client stays with Contoso Water. Small problems in this period, such as a missed delivery or a cooler in the wrong place, are easy to fix if someone asks about them early.

The policy applies to every new client in the UK, the Netherlands and Germany. Sam Patel, Client Onboarding Lead in London, owns this policy. Account managers do the calls.

## 2 The welcome call: who and when

The account manager makes the welcome call within 5 working days of the first delivery. The welcome call is step 6, the last step, of client onboarding (policy CL-ONB-01).

The account manager calls the person who signed the contract. If the site contact is a different person, the account manager also calls the site contact, or invites both people to the same call.

The welcome call is a phone or video call of about 15 minutes. It is not done by email. If the client does not answer, the account manager tries again the next working day, and after a third try sends an email asking the client to choose a time.

The account manager books the welcome call in the CRM as a task on the day of the first delivery.

## 3 What to cover in the welcome call

The account manager follows the welcome call checklist in the CRM. In every welcome call, the account manager:

- Thanks the client and asks how the installation went.
- Checks that every cooler is in the right place and working.
- Checks that the delivery day and time suit the office.
- Confirms the billing contact and when the first invoice will arrive.
- Explains how to reach the service desk: service@contoso-water.example or 0800 123 4567.
- Explains how to order extra bottles or paper cups.
- Asks whether anything is still unclear.

The account manager writes a short summary of the welcome call in the CRM on the same day, and copies it to the onboarding ticket in Jira project ONB. Sam Patel then closes the onboarding ticket.

## 4 The 30-day check-in

About 30 days after the first delivery, the account manager makes a short check-in call to the new client. The CRM creates this task on its own when the welcome call is logged.

In the 30-day check-in, the account manager asks:

- Have the weekly deliveries come on the agreed day?
- Is the number of bottles right, or is the office running out or keeping too many?
- Has the first invoice arrived, and is it correct?
- Has the client contacted the service desk, and was the problem solved?

Before the call, the account manager looks at the client record in the CRM for any missed deliveries, service desk cases or unpaid invoices, so the call can deal with them directly.

## 5 The 90-day review

About 90 days after the first delivery, the account manager holds a 90-day review with the new client. For clients with more than 10 coolers, the 90-day review is a visit to the office. For smaller clients, it is a call.

In the 90-day review, the account manager:

- Goes through the first three months: deliveries, service desk cases and invoices.
- Asks the client to score Contoso Water from 1 to 10.
- Checks whether the office needs more coolers, fewer coolers or a different cooler type.
- Confirms the account manager remains the main contact for the contract.

The account manager records the score and a short summary in the CRM. After the 90-day review, the client is looked after like every other client.

## 6 When a new client is unhappy

If a new client is unhappy at the welcome call, the 30-day check-in or the 90-day review, the account manager acts the same day.

- A delivery or cooler problem: the account manager opens a case with the service desk and tells the client the case number.
- An invoice problem: the account manager sends the details to Finance through the client record in the CRM.
- A formal complaint: the account manager logs it as a complaint in the CRM so that it follows the complaints process.

The account manager calls the client again when the problem is solved, to check the client agrees it is solved.

A new client who scores Contoso Water 6 or lower at the 90-day review is added to Sam Patel's list. Sam calls the client within 5 working days.

## 7 What the client onboarding team reports

Every month, Sam Patel reports on the first 90 days of all new clients to the Chief Executive, Maria Lopez. The report shows:

- How many welcome calls were made within 5 working days of the first delivery.
- How many 30-day check-ins and 90-day reviews took place on time.
- The average 90-day score, by office: London, Utrecht and Cologne.
- New clients who are unhappy, and what is being done.

Account managers keep the CRM up to date so that this report is correct. A call that is not in the CRM counts as not made.
