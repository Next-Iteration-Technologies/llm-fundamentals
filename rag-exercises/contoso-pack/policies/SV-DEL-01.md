# SV-DEL-01 Delivery areas, days and times

## 1 Purpose
This document explains where Contoso Water delivers, on which days, and at what times. It is written for our clients and for our own team, so both read the same rules.

Contoso Water rents water coolers to offices and refills them. Bottle coolers need a full 19-litre bottle swapped for an empty one every week. Plumbed-in coolers need no bottles, but they still get regular visits for cleaning and filter changes.

If anything in this document is unclear, clients can contact the service desk at service@contoso-water.example or 0800 123 4567. The owner of this document is Lee Wong, Delivery Operations Manager, London.

## 2 Where Contoso Water delivers
Contoso Water delivers from three offices. Each office has a small warehouse and its own delivery vans and drivers.

- **London** (head office, UK) delivers to offices in London and the surrounding area.
- **Utrecht** (Netherlands) delivers to offices in Utrecht and the surrounding area.
- **Cologne** (Germany) delivers to offices in Cologne and the surrounding area.

A client is served by the office nearest to them. For example, Fabrikam in London is served by the London warehouse, and Tailspin in Cologne is served by the Cologne warehouse.

If a client opens a new site outside these areas, the account manager checks with Lee Wong whether a van can reach it on a regular route before the new site is added to the contract.

## 3 Delivery days
Contoso Water delivers Monday to Friday. There are no deliveries on Saturdays, Sundays or public holidays.

Every bottle cooler client has one fixed delivery day each week. The delivery day is agreed when the client is onboarded and is written in the welcome letter. For example, a client may have "every Tuesday" as its delivery day.

- The fixed day stays the same every week, so the office knows when to expect the driver.
- A client can ask to change its delivery day. The service desk agrees the new day within 2 working days and confirms it by email.
- Plumbed-in cooler clients do not have a weekly delivery day. Their visits are booked for cleaning, filter changes and repairs.

## 4 Delivery times
Deliveries take place between 07:00 and 16:00, Monday to Friday. Drivers plan their routes in the morning, so the exact arrival time can change from week to week.

Contoso Water does not promise an exact time of arrival. A client who needs the driver to come in a narrower window, for example because the loading bay is only open in the morning, should tell the service desk. The window is added to the client's access instructions and the route is planned around it where possible.

If a driver is running more than one hour late, the driver or the service desk calls the client's site contact to let them know.

## 5 Public holidays and delivery days
Contoso Water does not deliver on public holidays in the UK, the Netherlands or Germany. Each office follows the public holidays of its own country.

When a client's fixed delivery day falls on a public holiday, the delivery moves to the next working day. For example, if a client in London has Monday deliveries and Monday is a bank holiday, the driver comes on Tuesday.

The service desk sends clients a short email in the week before a public holiday to confirm the moved date. Clients who need extra bottles before a long weekend should order them at least 2 working days in advance.

## 6 What a client needs to do on delivery day
A delivery only works if the driver can get in and leave the bottles. On the delivery day, the client should:

- Make sure the loading bay, lift and entrance named in the access instructions are open between 07:00 and 16:00.
- Have someone at the office who can sign for the delivery.
- Keep the area around the cooler clear, so the driver can swap the bottle safely.
- Put empty bottles together in one place, near the cooler or at the agreed collection point.

A 19-litre bottle weighs about 19 kg. Drivers use a trolley and never carry more than one bottle by hand up stairs, so a working lift makes deliveries to upper floors much quicker.

## 7 Changing the delivery address or access instructions
Clients must tell Contoso Water when their delivery address or access details change. This includes a new loading bay, new opening hours, a new door code or a new site contact.

To make a change, the client emails service@contoso-water.example or calls 0800 123 4567 at least 2 working days before the next delivery. The service desk updates the client record and the driver's route.

If the address is not updated, the driver may not be able to deliver. The delivery is then recorded as missed and the service desk contacts the client to rebook it.

## 8 Ordering extra bottles and supplies
Bottle cooler rental includes 4 bottles a week. Clients who need more can order extra bottles (WC-400) at £6.50 or €7.50 per bottle, per delivery. Paper cups (WC-420) can be ordered at £12 or €14 per box of 1,000.

Extra bottles and cups are delivered within 2 working days of the order. Where possible, they come with the next regular delivery.

To order, the client emails service@contoso-water.example or calls 0800 123 4567. Please give the company name, the delivery address and the number of bottles or boxes needed.
