# HR-SIC-01 Sick leave and reporting absence

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains what Contoso Water employees must do when they are ill and cannot work. It covers how to report a sick day, when you need a doctor's note, how sick pay works and how we help you come back to work.

The policy applies to all employees in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands and Germany. The basic rules are the same in every country. Some rules are different because each country has its own law. Those rules are in clearly labelled sections for the UK, the Netherlands and Germany.

Priya Shah, HR Manager, owns this policy. In the Netherlands, Emma de Vries, HR and office manager in Utrecht, is the local contact.

## 2 How to report that you are sick (all countries)

If you are ill and cannot work, you must **call your manager before 09:30 on the first day**. This rule applies in all three countries.

- **Call, do not text.** A text message, chat message or mail is not enough. Your manager needs to talk to you to plan cover.
- If your manager does not answer, call their deputy or the office number. For drivers and warehouse staff, call the warehouse team lead or Lee Wong, Delivery Operations Manager.
- Drivers on an early shift should call as soon as they know, and before their shift starts if they can. A late call can mean clients miss their delivery.

Tell your manager roughly how long you expect to be off and if any urgent work needs to be handed over. You do not have to say what illness you have.

After the call, record the sick day in **PeopleHub**, the HR app, under **Time off > Report sickness**. If you are too ill, your manager can do it for you.

## 3 Keeping in touch and coming back (all countries)

While you are off sick, keep your manager informed. If you are off for more than 3 days, agree with your manager when they will call you next. A short call once a week is normal.

When you are better, tell your manager the day before you come back. Then close the sickness in PeopleHub under **Time off > Back at work**.

After a sickness of more than 2 weeks, your manager will have a short return-to-work talk with you on your first day back. The talk is to help, not to judge. You can agree on:

- starting with shorter days for a while,
- lighter tasks, for example no heavy lifting for drivers and warehouse staff,
- support from HR.

If you are often ill for short periods, for example 4 or more separate sick periods in 12 months, your manager and HR will ask to talk with you about how we can help.

## 4 United Kingdom rules

These rules apply to Contoso Water employees in the **United Kingdom**.

- **Days 1 to 7:** you do not need a doctor's note. When you return, fill in the self-certification form in PeopleHub.
- **From day 8:** you need a **fit note** from your doctor. Take a photo of it and upload it in PeopleHub within 2 working days of receiving it.
- If the fit note says you "may be fit for work" with changes, your manager will talk to you about what changes are possible.

**Sick pay in the UK:** Contoso Water pays your full normal pay for up to **20 working days** of sickness in each leave year (1 January to 31 December). After that you get Statutory Sick Pay, as UK law sets out. Long cases are looked at one by one by Priya Shah.

Example: Tom is ill for 10 working days in March. He calls his manager at 07:00 on day 1. He gets a fit note for day 8 onwards and uploads it in PeopleHub. He is paid in full.

## 5 Netherlands rules

These rules apply to Contoso Water employees in the **Netherlands**.

- Call your manager before 09:30 on the first day, as in all countries.
- **From day 1**, your sickness is also reported to the **company doctor (bedrijfsarts)**. Emma de Vries in Utrecht does this for you once you have reported sick in PeopleHub.
- In the Netherlands you do not need a note from your own doctor. The company doctor may call you or invite you to a meeting. You must go to that meeting.
- Do not tell your manager what illness you have. Medical questions are only for the company doctor.

**Sick pay in the Netherlands:** Dutch law requires at least 70% of pay for up to 2 years. Contoso Water tops this up to **100% in the first year** of sickness and pays 70% in the second year.

If you are sick for a long time, Dutch law sets out steps for getting back to work. Emma de Vries and the company doctor will guide you through them.

## 6 Germany rules

These rules apply to Contoso Water employees in **Germany**.

- Call your manager before 09:30 on the first day, as in all countries.
- **From day 4** of sickness, you need a **doctor's certificate**. Your doctor sends it to your health insurer, and Contoso Water receives it electronically. You must still tell your manager how long the certificate lasts.
- If the certificate is extended, tell your manager on the last day of the old certificate.

**Sick pay in Germany:** Contoso Water pays your full normal pay for the first **6 weeks** of each illness, as German law requires. After 6 weeks, your health insurer pays sickness benefit.

Example: Lukas, a driver in Cologne, is ill from Monday. He calls his manager at 06:30. On Thursday, day 4, he sees his doctor and gets a certificate until the following Wednesday. He tells his manager the end date the same day.

## 7 Sick children, doctor visits and other absence (all countries)

Some absence is not sickness, but you must still report it in the same way: call your manager first, then record it in PeopleHub.

- **A sick child or family emergency:** you can take time off to deal with the emergency. Choose **Family emergency** in PeopleHub. The local law of each country decides whether it is paid.
- **Doctor or dentist visits:** try to book them outside working hours or at the start or end of the day. If that is not possible, agree the time with your manager.
- **Hospital or planned operations:** tell your manager as soon as you have the date, so cover can be planned.

Never stay away from work without telling anyone. Not reporting an absence by 09:30 is treated as unauthorised absence and may be unpaid.
