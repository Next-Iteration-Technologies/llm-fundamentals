# CL-DOC-01 Documents we need from a new client

## 1 Purpose

This policy lists the documents and details Contoso Water needs from every new client before the coolers can be installed. It also says who collects them, where they are stored and what to do when something is missing.

The policy applies to every new client in the UK, the Netherlands and Germany. It also applies when an existing client opens a new office that needs its own delivery address.

Sam Patel, Client Onboarding Lead in London, owns this policy. Account managers collect the documents. The full onboarding process is in policy CL-ONB-01.

## 2 The list of documents from a new client

Contoso Water needs six things from every new client:

- The signed contract, signed by the client and by Contoso Water.
- The company registration number (Companies House in the UK, the Chamber of Commerce in the Netherlands, the commercial register in Germany).
- The VAT number.
- A billing contact: name, email address and phone number of the person who receives invoices.
- A site contact: name, email address and phone number of the person at the office who lets drivers in and signs for deliveries.
- The delivery address and access instructions: loading bay, lift, opening hours, and anything else a driver needs to know to get in.

The new client onboarding cannot reach installation until all six are in the CRM. The signed contract is always the first document; the onboarding does not start without it.

## 3 How the account manager asks for the documents

The account manager sends the new client the document request on the same day the signed contract arrives. The account manager uses the "New client documents" email template in the CRM. The template lists the five remaining items and explains why each one is needed.

The client can reply by email or fill in the short online form linked in the email. Most clients answer within 2 working days.

The account manager does not ask for the same detail twice. If the client already gave the VAT number or the billing contact during the sale, the account manager copies it from the sales notes into the CRM and leaves it out of the request.

## 4 Where the new client documents are stored

All new client documents and details go into the client record in the CRM. Nothing is kept only in an email inbox or on a personal drive.

- The signed contract is saved as a scanned file in the "Contracts" folder of the client record.
- The company registration number and the VAT number go into the matching fields on the client record.
- The billing contact and the site contact are added as contacts on the client record, each with the right role.
- The delivery address and access instructions go into the "Delivery" tab, which drivers see on their route list.

When all six items are in the CRM, the account manager ticks the "Client documents complete" line on the onboarding checklist in Jira project ONB.

## 5 Why each client document matters

Each of the six documents is needed for a clear reason:

- The signed contract fixes the products, prices and contract length. Without it nothing is delivered.
- The company registration number lets Finance run the credit check (policy CL-CRD-01) and confirms the company exists.
- The VAT number must appear on every invoice to a business client.
- The billing contact makes sure invoices reach the right person and are paid on time.
- The site contact is the person drivers call when they arrive and the person who signs for deliveries.
- The access instructions stop missed deliveries. A driver who cannot find the loading bay, or arrives when the building is closed, cannot deliver.

## 6 Delivery address and access instructions in detail

Access instructions are the details most often missing from a new client. The account manager checks that the client has answered each of these questions:

- Where should the van park? Is there a loading bay, and does it need to be booked?
- Is there a lift that can take a trolley with bottles? On which floors does it stop?
- What are the building opening hours, Monday to Friday?
- Does the driver need a pass, a code or a name at reception?
- Who signs for the delivery if the site contact is away?

The surveyor checks the same points at the site survey (policy CL-SIT-01) and updates the "Delivery" tab in the CRM if the client's answers were wrong or incomplete.

## 7 When a client document is missing

If any of the six items is still missing 3 working days after the signed contract, the account manager calls the client and asks for it by phone. The account manager writes the call on the onboarding ticket in Jira project ONB.

If the item is still missing after 5 working days, the account manager adds Sam Patel to the onboarding ticket. Sam decides whether the installation date must move.

Some gaps stop the onboarding completely:

- No company registration number: Finance cannot run the credit check, so the installation cannot be booked.
- No site contact or no access instructions: the delivery team will not book the installation.

A missing billing contact does not stop the installation. Invoices go to the person who signed the contract until the billing contact is known.
