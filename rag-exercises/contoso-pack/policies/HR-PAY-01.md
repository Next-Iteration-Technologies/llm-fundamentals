# HR-PAY-01 Pay and payslips

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains when and how Contoso Water employees are paid, where to find payslips, and what to do if something on your payslip is wrong. It applies to all employees in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands and Germany.

UK employees are paid in pounds (£). Employees in the Netherlands and Germany are paid in euros (€).

Contoso Water uses an external payroll provider, **PayRight**, to calculate pay and make payslips. HR sends PayRight the changes each month, such as new joiners, overtime and pay rises.

This policy does not contain salary figures. Priya Shah, HR Manager, owns this policy.

## 2 Pay day

All Contoso Water employees are paid **monthly, on the 25th of each month**.

- If the 25th is a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday, you are paid on the **last working day before** the 25th.
- The money is paid into the bank account you gave in PeopleHub, the HR app.
- It normally reaches your account the same day, but some banks take until the end of the day.

Examples:

- 25 October 2026 is a Sunday, so pay arrives on Friday 23 October 2026.
- 25 December is Christmas Day, a public holiday in all three countries, so December pay always arrives before Christmas.

You are paid for the whole calendar month in which the pay day falls. Overtime and expenses approved by your manager are added to the next pay day.

## 3 Your payslip in the PayRight app

Your payslips are in the **PayRight app**, on your phone or in a web browser. There are no paper payslips.

How to see your payslip:

1. Open the PayRight app and sign in with your work mail address.
2. Choose **Payslips**. The newest payslip appears about **2 working days before pay day**.
3. Tap the month to open it, or download it as a file.

You receive your PayRight login in your first week at Contoso Water. If you cannot sign in, contact the IT helpdesk at it@contoso-water.example or extension 200.

Your year-end tax documents are also in the PayRight app. Keep copies of your payslips; you may need them for a mortgage or a rental contract.

## 4 What is on your payslip

Your Contoso Water payslip shows:

- your **gross pay**: your monthly salary before tax,
- **extras**: paid overtime for drivers and warehouse staff, allowances, and expenses paid back,
- **deductions**: income tax, social security, pension contributions and any other agreed deductions,
- your **net pay**: the amount paid into your bank account,
- **year-to-date totals**.

The names of taxes and deductions are different in each country, because each country has its own tax system. In the Netherlands, the payslip in May also shows the yearly holiday allowance.

If you do not understand a line on your payslip, ask HR. We are happy to explain it.

## 5 What to do if your payslip is wrong

If you think something on your payslip is wrong, for example missing overtime, a wrong tax code or a pay rise that is not included, **tell HR**.

How to report a payslip error:

1. Send a mail to **hr@contoso-water.example** with the subject "Payslip question" and your name.
2. Say which month and which line you think is wrong, and what you expected.
3. Attach a screenshot of the payslip if you can.

HR checks your question and replies within **5 working days**. If there is an error, HR arranges the correction. Small corrections are made on the next payslip. If the error means you are missing a lot of money, HR can arrange an extra payment sooner.

Example: Aisha's September payslip does not show 6 hours of approved overtime. She mails HR on 26 September. HR checks the hours in PeopleHub and the overtime is added to her October pay.

## 6 Changing your personal details

Your pay depends on correct personal details. Keep them up to date in **PeopleHub**, the HR app:

- **Bank account:** change it in PeopleHub under **My details > Bank**. Changes made by the **10th** of the month are used for that month's pay. Later changes are used the next month.
- **Address and name:** change them in PeopleHub as soon as possible, because they are printed on your tax documents.
- **Tax information:** in the UK, HR updates your tax code when the tax office sends a new one. In the Netherlands and Germany, tell HR about changes such as a new tax class.

HR will never ask you for your bank details by mail or phone. If you get such a message, do not reply and forward it to it@contoso-water.example.

## 7 Pay rises, advances and overpayments

**Pay rises** at Contoso Water take effect on 1 July after the April review. You see the new amount on your July payslip.

**Salary advances:** in an emergency, you can ask HR for an advance of part of your next pay. Advances are paid back from your next pay and need approval from Priya Shah, HR Manager.

**Overpayments:** if you are paid too much by mistake, tell HR. You must pay it back. HR agrees a fair plan with you, for example spread over 3 months.

Salary information is private. Do not share colleagues' pay details. Salary bands for roles are kept by HR and are confidential.
