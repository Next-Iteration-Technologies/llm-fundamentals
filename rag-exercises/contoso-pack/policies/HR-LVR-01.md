# HR-LVR-01 Leaving Contoso Water

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains what happens when an employee leaves Contoso Water. It covers notice periods, how to resign, returning company equipment, final pay and the exit interview. It applies to employees in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands and Germany.

People leave for many reasons: a new job, a move, retirement or the end of a fixed-term contract. Whatever the reason, we want leaving to be fair, clear and friendly, and we want clients to keep getting good service.

If your contract or national law says something different from this policy, the contract or law applies. Priya Shah, HR Manager, owns this policy.

## 2 Notice periods

The notice period is the time between handing in your notice and your last working day. At Contoso Water the notice periods are:

- **In the first 2 years of employment** (including the 6-month probation): **1 month**.
- **After 2 years of employment:** **3 months**.

The same notice periods apply when Contoso Water ends the employment, unless national law requires a longer period. In Germany and the Netherlands, the law can give long-serving employees a longer notice period from the employer; HR checks this in every case.

Notice starts on the day HR receives it. Example: Tom, a driver who joined 5 years ago, hands in his notice on 10 March. His notice period is 3 months, so his last working day is 9 June.

You and your manager can agree a shorter notice period. This must be confirmed by HR in writing.

## 3 How to resign

If you decide to leave Contoso Water:

1. Tell your manager first, in person or by phone.
2. Then send your notice in writing to your manager and to hr@contoso-water.example. A short mail or letter is enough: your name, that you are resigning, and the date you think will be your last day.
3. In **Germany**, notice must be on **paper with your own signature**. A mail is not valid in Germany. Give or post the signed letter to HR.
4. HR confirms your last working day in writing within 5 working days.

After that, your manager and you agree a handover plan. For drivers this covers routes and client access instructions; for account managers it covers each client and any open contract or credit check.

## 4 Annual leave and working during notice

During your notice period at Contoso Water you work as normal, unless your manager agrees otherwise.

- **Annual leave:** check your remaining balance in PeopleHub, the HR app. You should plan to take your remaining leave during your notice period, agreed with your manager. Leave not taken is paid with your final pay; if you took more leave than you earned, the extra is taken off your final pay. The rules of your country's annual leave policy apply.
- **Handover:** write down what you do, open tasks and important contacts. Your manager will tell you who takes over.
- **Garden leave:** in some cases, for example when someone joins a competitor, Contoso Water may ask you to stay at home on full pay during your notice period. You are still an employee until your last day.

Please keep client information private, during and after your notice period, as the code of conduct HR-CON-01 asks.

## 5 Returning company equipment

On your last working day at Contoso Water, you must return all company property. Your manager checks it off on the leaver checklist in PeopleHub.

Return:

- laptop and charger, company phone and headset,
- ID badge and office or warehouse keys,
- van keys and the **fuel card**, for drivers,
- safety shoes, high-visibility vest and uniform, for drivers and warehouse staff,
- any paper documents, client files or samples.

Do not delete files from your laptop. The IT helpdesk wipes it safely after you leave. Personal files should be copied off before your last week.

If you work from home and cannot come in, ask HR for a prepaid parcel. Equipment not returned within **10 working days** after your last day may be charged to you from your final pay, where the law allows.

## 6 Final pay

Your final pay at Contoso Water is paid on the normal pay day, the **25th of the month** (or the last working day before), after your last working day. If you leave late in the month, it may be paid the following month.

Your final pay includes:

- your normal pay up to your last working day,
- payment for any annual leave you have not taken,
- approved overtime and expenses not yet paid,
- minus any leave taken but not yet earned, and any equipment not returned.

Your final payslip appears in the PayRight app, as usual. You keep access to the PayRight app for **12 months** after leaving, so download your payslips and year-end tax forms in that time. In the UK you receive your P45 form after your final pay.

If something looks wrong on your final payslip, tell HR at hr@contoso-water.example.

## 7 Exit interview

Every employee leaving Contoso Water is invited to an **exit interview** with HR in their last 2 weeks. It takes about 30 minutes, in person or by video call.

In the exit interview we ask:

- why you are leaving,
- what you liked about working at Contoso Water,
- what we could do better, for example about pay, workload, tools or managers.

The exit interview is voluntary, but we really value it. Your answers are kept private within HR. Priya Shah shares a summary without names with Maria Lopez, Chief Executive, twice a year.

You can also give feedback in writing, through the leaver form in PeopleHub, if you prefer.
