# HR-IT-01 Laptops, phones and passwords

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains, in simple words, how Contoso Water employees should look after their work laptops and phones and keep their passwords safe. It is written for everyone, not for computer experts. It applies to all employees in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands and Germany.

Laptops and phones hold client names, addresses, contracts and prices. If a device is lost or a password is stolen, this information could fall into the wrong hands. A few simple habits protect you, our clients and Contoso Water.

The IT helpdesk helps with all questions: it@contoso-water.example or phone extension 200, Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00. Priya Shah, HR Manager, owns this policy together with the IT helpdesk.

## 2 Your work laptop and phone

Contoso Water gives you the devices you need for your job:

- **Office staff** get a laptop and a headset. Some roles, such as account managers, also get a company phone.
- **Drivers** get a company phone with the delivery app, for routes, delivery notes and clocking in.
- **Warehouse team leads** get a phone and use the warehouse tablet.

Your work devices belong to Contoso Water. Rules for using them:

- Use them mainly for work. Short personal use, such as checking the news at lunch, is fine.
- Do not let family or friends use them.
- Do not install apps or programs yourself. Ask the IT helpdesk.
- Never leave a laptop or phone visible in a car or van. Take it with you or lock it in the boot.

If your device is broken, call extension 200. The IT helpdesk normally gives you a replacement within 2 working days.

## 3 Passwords: how to make a good one

A good password is long and easy for you to remember, but hard for others to guess. At Contoso Water your password must be **at least 12 characters** long.

The easiest way: **three random words**, for example "kettle-orange-bicycle". Add a number if you like. Do not use:

- your name, your children's or pet's names, or your birthday,
- "Contoso", "water" or "password",
- the same password you use for private websites.

Never share your password, not even with your manager or the IT helpdesk. The IT helpdesk will **never** ask you for your password.

Write your password down only if you must, and keep the paper at home, never with the laptop. You change your password only if you think someone else knows it.

## 4 The sign-in code on your phone

When you sign in to your work account, PeopleHub or the PayRight app, you are often asked for a **code from your phone** as well as your password. This second step keeps your account safe even if someone steals your password.

- Set up the sign-in app on your phone in your first week. The IT helpdesk helps you.
- Only approve a sign-in request when **you** are signing in. If your phone asks you to approve a sign-in you did not start, press **Deny** and call extension 200.
- If you get a new phone, call the IT helpdesk **before** you reset or give away the old one.

Example: Noor Haddad gets a sign-in request at 22:00 while she is watching television. She did not try to sign in. She presses Deny and calls the IT helpdesk the next morning, who reset her password.

## 5 Suspicious mails and messages

Criminals send fake mails and text messages to steal passwords or money. These can look like they come from a colleague, a client, a delivery company, PayRight or a bank.

Warning signs:

- the message is urgent, for example "pay today" or "your account will close",
- it asks for your password, bank details or a payment,
- the sender address looks strange, for example not ending in @contoso-water.example,
- there is an unexpected attachment or link.

What to do:

1. Do not click links or open attachments.
2. Forward the message to **it@contoso-water.example**.
3. Delete it.

If you already clicked or typed your password, call extension 200 straight away. You will not be in trouble for reporting it.

## 6 Lost or stolen devices

If your work laptop or phone is lost or stolen, **call the IT helpdesk on extension 200 straight away**, or mail it@contoso-water.example from another device. Outside office hours, leave a voice message and also tell your manager.

The IT helpdesk can lock and wipe the device from a distance, so nobody can read the information on it. The sooner you call, the safer the information is.

If the device was stolen, also report it to the police and send the police reference to the IT helpdesk.

Example: Pieter, a driver in Utrecht, leaves his company phone in a client's lift at 11:00. He realises at 11:20, borrows a colleague's phone and calls extension 200. The phone is locked within minutes. The client finds it later and Pieter gets it back.

## 7 Working away from the office

Many Contoso Water employees work in vans, at client sites or at home. Keep information safe wherever you work:

- **Lock your screen** whenever you step away: press the Windows key and L together.
- In trains, cafés and other public places, sit where others cannot see your screen, and do not discuss clients on the phone.
- Use your home network or your phone's hotspot. Avoid free public wifi in cafés and hotels.
- Do not print client documents at home or in hotels.
- Save work files in the company's shared folders, not only on the laptop. Then nothing is lost if the laptop breaks.

Leaving Contoso Water? Give back all devices on your last day, as HR-LVR-01 explains.
