# SV-SLA-01 Service levels

## 1 Purpose
This document sets out the service levels Contoso Water promises to every client. A service level is a time limit: how quickly we deliver, repair, or respond.

The same service levels apply in the UK, the Netherlands and Germany. They are part of every rental contract, and clients can read this document.

If Contoso Water misses a service level, the client can ask for a credit. Credits are explained in SV-CMP-01 Complaints, refunds and credits.

The owner of this document is Lee Wong, Delivery Operations Manager, London. Questions go to the service desk at service@contoso-water.example or 0800 123 4567.

## 2 How working days are counted
Contoso Water service levels are counted in working days. A working day is Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in the client's country.

The count starts on the working day after the client's request reaches the service desk. For example:

- A client reports a broken cooler on Monday at 11:00. Day 1 is Tuesday, day 2 is Wednesday, day 3 is Thursday. The repair or swap must be done by the end of Thursday.
- A request made on Friday at 15:00 starts counting on Monday.

The only service level counted in hours is the leak response, because a leak can damage the client's office.

## 3 Regular bottle deliveries
Bottle cooler clients get a full delivery once a week on their fixed delivery day. Rental of a bottle cooler includes 4 bottles a week.

- Deliveries take place Monday to Friday, between 07:00 and 16:00.
- The fixed delivery day is agreed at onboarding and does not change unless the client asks.
- If the delivery day falls on a public holiday, the delivery moves to the next working day.

If the driver cannot get in, for example because the loading bay is closed, the delivery is recorded as missed and rebooked within 2 working days. Missed deliveries are not charged.

## 4 Extra bottles and supplies
Contoso Water delivers extra bottles within 2 working days of the order. This service level also applies to paper cups.

- Extra 19-litre bottles (WC-400): £6.50 or €7.50 per bottle, per delivery.
- Paper cups, box of 1,000 (WC-420): £12 or €14.

When the next regular delivery is within 2 working days, the extra bottles usually travel with it. For example, a client with Thursday deliveries who orders extra bottles on Tuesday gets them on Thursday. Clients order extra bottles from the service desk at service@contoso-water.example or 0800 123 4567.

## 5 Broken coolers
Contoso Water repairs or swaps a broken cooler within 3 working days of the report.

A cooler counts as broken when it does not give cold water, does not give hot water on a hot and cold model, makes a loud noise, or shows an error light. The service technician decides on site whether to repair the cooler or replace it with a working one of the same model.

There is no charge for repairing or swapping a cooler that broke during normal use. When reporting a broken cooler, please give the company name, the floor, and where the cooler stands.

## 6 Leaks
A leaking cooler is the most urgent kind of call. The client should switch off the cooler at the plug, close the water valve if it is a plumbed-in cooler, and call the service desk on 0800 123 4567.

- In the cities of London, Utrecht and Cologne, someone from Contoso Water is on site within 4 hours.
- Elsewhere, someone is on site the next working day.

The 4-hour response applies during delivery hours, Monday to Friday, 07:00 to 16:00. Please call rather than email for a leak, so the service desk can send someone at once.

## 7 Cleaning and filter changes
Hygiene visits are included in the rental price and follow a fixed schedule.

- Every cooler is sanitised every 3 months.
- Plumbed-in coolers get a filter change every 6 months.
- A taste or smell complaint gets a deep clean and filter change within 2 working days, free of charge.

The service desk books the regular visits and tells the client the date in advance. Extra filter changes (WC-410, £25 or €29) and extra deep clean visits (WC-450, £40 or €46) can be ordered outside the included ones.

## 8 Complaints and service desk response
Contoso Water acknowledges every complaint within 1 working day and sends a full answer within 5 working days.

Clients can complain by email to service@contoso-water.example or by phone on 0800 123 4567. The account manager is kept informed of every complaint about their clients.

When a service level in this document is missed, the client can ask for a credit of one week of rental per breach, up to a maximum of one month of rental per quarter. The full process is in SV-CMP-01 Complaints, refunds and credits.
