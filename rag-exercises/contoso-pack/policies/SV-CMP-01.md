# SV-CMP-01 Complaints, refunds and credits

## 1 Purpose
This document explains how Contoso Water handles complaints from clients, and when a client gets a refund or a credit on the next invoice.

It applies to all clients in the UK, the Netherlands and Germany, and to every member of staff who takes a complaint. Clients can read this document. A complaint is any message from a client saying that our service, a cooler, a driver or an invoice was not as it should be.

The owner of this document is Lee Wong, Delivery Operations Manager, London. The service levels that credits are based on are in SV-SLA-01 Service levels.

## 2 How a client makes a complaint
A client can make a complaint to Contoso Water in two ways:

- By email to service@contoso-water.example.
- By phone to the service desk on 0800 123 4567, Monday to Friday.

A complaint can also be given to a driver or to the account manager. Staff who receive a complaint this way pass it to the service desk on the same day, so that it is recorded.

To help us answer quickly, the client should give the company name, the site address, the date of the problem, and what went wrong. Photos are welcome, for example of a damaged bottle or a leak.

## 3 Response times for complaints
Contoso Water acknowledges every complaint within 1 working day. The acknowledgement is an email that confirms we have the complaint and names the person handling it.

Contoso Water sends a full answer within 5 working days. The answer says what happened, what we have done to fix it, and whether a credit or refund applies.

If a complaint needs more time, for example because a supplier must be asked, the service desk tells the client before the 5 working days are up and gives a new date.

Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in the client's country.

## 4 Who handles a complaint
The service desk records every complaint and handles most of them directly.

- Complaints about deliveries, drivers or missed visits go to Lee Wong, Delivery Operations Manager.
- Complaints about invoices or prices go to Jonas Becker, Finance Controller.
- Complaints about the contract or the relationship go to the client's account manager. For example, Noor Haddad handles complaints from Fabrikam and Woodgrove.

The account manager is always told about a complaint from their client, even when the service desk solves it. Complaints that are not solved within 5 working days are raised with Lee Wong.

## 5 Credits for missed service levels
When Contoso Water misses a service level in SV-SLA-01, the client is entitled to a credit.

- The credit is one week of rental per breach.
- The maximum credit is one month of rental per quarter.
- The credit is based on the monthly rental of the cooler affected.

For example, Tailspin rents a WC-250 plumbed-in cooler at €41 a month. If a broken WC-250 is not repaired or swapped within 3 working days, Tailspin can claim one week of rental for that cooler.

Credits appear on the next invoice. Clients ask for a credit through the service desk, or the service desk offers it when it sees the breach.

## 6 What does not lead to a credit
Some situations do not give a right to a credit, because the service level was not missed by Contoso Water.

- A missed delivery because the client's loading bay was closed, nobody could sign, or the access instructions were out of date. Missed deliveries are never charged, but they do not lead to a credit either.
- A delay caused by the client asking for a later date.
- Damage caused by moving a cooler without our help.

A van breakdown or a mistake by our driver is our responsibility. If it causes a service level to be missed, the normal credit applies.

## 7 Refunds
A refund means money paid back to the client, not a credit on a future invoice. Contoso Water gives a refund in these cases:

- The client was charged twice for the same item.
- The client was charged for something that was never delivered, for example extra bottles (WC-400) that did not arrive.
- The contract has ended and a credit is still open, so there is no next invoice.

Refunds are approved by Jonas Becker, Finance Controller, and paid to the client's bank account within 10 working days of approval. In the UK refunds are paid in GBP; in the Netherlands and Germany in EUR.

## 8 Learning from complaints
Contoso Water reviews complaints every month to find problems that repeat.

Lee Wong reviews all complaints from the month with the service desk in the first week of the next month. The review looks at the number of complaints, the reasons, and the response times. Repeated problems, such as one route with many late deliveries, lead to a change in how we work.

Clients are never named outside the team that handles their account. Complaint records are kept for the contract plus 90 days after it ends, unless a legal claim is open.
