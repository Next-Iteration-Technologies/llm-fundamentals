# CL-ONB-01 Client onboarding: from signed contract to first delivery

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains how Contoso Water takes a new client from a signed contract to the first delivery of water. It applies to every new client in the UK, the Netherlands and Germany, for bottle coolers and plumbed-in coolers.

Client onboarding has one main target: the coolers are installed within 10 working days of the signed contract. The 10 working days start on the day after Contoso Water receives the contract signed by both sides.

Sam Patel, Client Onboarding Lead in London, owns this policy. Account managers, Finance, the delivery teams and the service desk all have a part in client onboarding. Each part is described below.

## 2 The onboarding steps in order

Every client onboarding follows the same six steps, in this order:

1. Signed contract received.
2. Credit check by Finance (policy CL-CRD-01), when the client needs one.
3. Site survey at the client's office (policy CL-SIT-01).
4. Installation booked with the client.
5. Installation and first delivery.
6. Welcome call within 5 working days of the first delivery (policy CL-WEL-01).

A step does not start until the step before it is finished, with one exception: the site survey may be booked while the credit check is still running, but the installation may not be booked until the credit check result is known.

Each step is a line on the onboarding checklist in Jira project ONB. The person who finishes a step ticks it off and adds the date.

## 3 Step 1: the signed contract and the onboarding ticket

The account manager who won the client starts the onboarding on the day the signed contract arrives.

The account manager does three things on that day:

- Saves the signed contract in the client record in the CRM.
- Opens a new onboarding ticket in Jira project ONB. The ticket uses the onboarding checklist template, so all six steps appear automatically.
- Sends the client the list of documents Contoso Water needs (policy CL-DOC-01).

The onboarding ticket title is the client name and the office city, for example "Fabrikam, London". The account manager sets the due date to 10 working days after the contract date.

If a contract arrives without both signatures, the onboarding does not start. The account manager returns the contract to the client the same day and asks for the missing signature.

## 4 Step 2 and step 3: credit check and site survey

After the onboarding ticket is open, two things run side by side.

Credit check. The account manager asks Finance for a credit check when the client's expected spend is above £500 / €580 per month. The request goes through the credit check line on the onboarding ticket. Finance gives the result within 3 working days. The full rules are in policy CL-CRD-01.

Site survey. The account manager asks the delivery team in the right office (London, Utrecht or Cologne) to book a site survey. The surveyor visits the client's office and checks where each cooler can go. The surveyor uploads the survey report to the onboarding ticket on the same day as the visit. The full rules are in policy CL-SIT-01.

The site survey should take place by working day 4 so that the installation can still happen by working day 10.

## 5 Step 4 and step 5: installation and first delivery

When the credit check result and the site survey report are both on the onboarding ticket, the delivery team books the installation.

The delivery team agrees a date and a time slot with the client's site contact. Installations take place Monday to Friday, between 07:00 and 16:00. The delivery team writes the installation date on the onboarding ticket and in the CRM.

On the installation day the driver or installer:

- Installs each cooler where the site survey said it should go.
- For bottle coolers, delivers the first bottles at the same visit.
- For plumbed-in coolers, connects the cooler, runs water through it and checks for leaks.
- Asks the site contact to sign for the delivery.

The installation counts as done when the client has signed. The delivery team ticks the step on the onboarding ticket.

## 6 Step 6: handing over to the account manager

The welcome call closes the client onboarding. The account manager makes the welcome call within 5 working days of the first delivery. The rules for the call are in policy CL-WEL-01.

After the welcome call, the account manager writes a short summary on the onboarding ticket: what the client liked, what went wrong, and anything still open. Sam Patel then closes the onboarding ticket.

From this point, the client is a normal client. Questions about deliveries, extra bottles or repairs go to the service desk at service@contoso-water.example or 0800 123 4567. The account manager stays the client's main contact for the contract and for invoices.

## 7 When client onboarding runs late

Sometimes client onboarding cannot meet the 10-working-day target. Common reasons are a late credit check, a survey that finds a problem with the site, or a client who cannot offer an installation date.

When a step is late, the person responsible for that step writes the reason on the onboarding ticket on the same day. If the installation will be later than working day 10, the account manager:

- Tells the client the new date, by phone and then in writing.
- Tells Sam Patel by adding him to the onboarding ticket.
- Changes the due date on the ticket and keeps the original date in the ticket comments.

Sam Patel reviews all late onboarding tickets every Monday. If a delay is caused by Contoso Water, Sam decides with the account manager whether to offer the client a goodwill gesture. Any price change must follow policy CL-PRC-01.

A client who asks for a later installation date on purpose does not count as late. The account manager notes "date chosen by client" on the onboarding ticket.
