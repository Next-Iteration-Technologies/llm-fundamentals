# HR-PAY-02 Salary bands

## 1 Purpose and confidentiality

This document sets out the salary bands for the main roles at Contoso Water in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands and Germany. A salary band is the range from the lowest to the highest yearly gross salary that Contoso Water pays for a role.

This document is **Confidential**. Only HR may see it: Priya Shah, HR Manager, and Emma de Vries, HR and office manager in Utrecht. Jonas Becker, Finance Controller, receives the figures for budgeting. Managers do not receive this document; HR tells a manager the band for a specific role when they hire or plan pay rises.

Do not forward, print or copy the salary bands. Employees can ask HR roughly where their own pay sits in their band, but not for other people's pay.

All amounts are yearly gross salaries for full-time work. Priya Shah owns this document.

## 2 Salary bands in the United Kingdom

These are the Contoso Water salary bands for the **United Kingdom**, in pounds, yearly gross, full time:

| Role | Lowest | Highest |
|---|---|---|
| Driver | £26,000 | £31,000 |
| Warehouse operative | £24,000 | £28,000 |
| Account manager | £38,000 | £48,000 |
| Team lead | £45,000 | £58,000 |

The team lead band covers team leads in the warehouse, the delivery team, the service desk and client onboarding in London.

Paid overtime for drivers and warehouse staff comes on top of the band. It is not counted when checking whether a salary is inside the band.

Part-time salaries in the UK are worked out in proportion to hours. Example: a warehouse operative working 30 of 40 hours with a full-time salary of £26,000 is paid £19,500 a year.

## 3 Salary bands in the Netherlands

These are the Contoso Water salary bands for the **Netherlands** (Utrecht office), in euros, yearly gross, full time. The amounts do not include the Dutch holiday allowance, which is paid on top each May.

| Role | Lowest | Highest |
|---|---|---|
| Driver | €30,000 | €36,000 |
| Warehouse operative | €28,000 | €33,000 |
| Account manager | €44,000 | €56,000 |
| Team lead | €52,000 | €67,000 |

Dutch salaries at Contoso Water are paid in 12 monthly parts, plus the holiday allowance. Paid overtime for drivers and warehouse staff comes on top of the band.

Example: a new driver in Utrecht with 3 years of experience elsewhere might start at €32,000, which is low in the band but leaves room for growth.

## 4 Salary bands in Germany

These are the Contoso Water salary bands for **Germany** (Cologne office), in euros, yearly gross, full time:

| Role | Lowest | Highest |
|---|---|---|
| Driver | €31,000 | €37,000 |
| Warehouse operative | €29,000 | €34,000 |
| Account manager | €45,000 | €57,000 |
| Team lead | €54,000 | €68,000 |

German salaries at Contoso Water are paid in 12 monthly parts. There is no separate Christmas bonus. Paid overtime for drivers and warehouse staff comes on top of the band.

Every salary in Germany must also be above the German legal minimum wage. HR checks this each January, when the minimum wage often changes.

## 5 Using the bands when hiring

When Contoso Water hires a new employee, the starting salary must be **inside the band** for the role and country.

- Most new employees start in the **lower half** of the band. This leaves room for pay rises.
- A start in the **upper half** needs approval from Priya Shah, HR Manager, and a written reason, for example rare experience.
- A salary **above the band** is never offered without approval from Maria Lopez, Chief Executive.

The hiring manager asks HR for the band before talking about money with a candidate. HR then makes the offer in writing.

Example: Lee Wong wants to hire a driver in London with 5 years of experience. HR suggests £28,000, within the £26,000–£31,000 band. Lee agrees and HR sends the offer.

## 6 Using the bands for pay rises

Pay rises at Contoso Water take effect on 1 July, after the April review. HR and Finance use the salary bands to make pay rises fair.

- An employee **low in the band** with a Strong or Good rating can get a larger rise, to move them towards the middle.
- An employee **near the top** of the band gets a smaller rise.
- An employee **at the top** of the band gets no rise in salary. Instead, HR may suggest a one-off payment, approved by Jonas Becker, Finance Controller, within the budget.
- A **promotion** moves the employee into the band of the new role. The new salary must be at least the lowest point of the new band.

Example: an account manager in London on £47,000 is near the top of the £38,000–£48,000 band. Even with a Strong rating, her rise is limited to £1,000 this year.

## 7 Reviewing the bands and other roles

HR reviews the Contoso Water salary bands **once a year, in May**, before pay rises are decided. Priya Shah compares the bands with market data for each country and proposes changes. Maria Lopez, Chief Executive, and Jonas Becker, Finance Controller, approve them.

If a band goes up, employees below the new lowest point are raised to it on 1 July.

Roles not listed in this document, such as service desk staff, finance and HR staff, and senior managers, do not yet have published bands. Their salaries are set one by one by HR and Finance. Bands for the service desk are planned for the 2027 review.
