# HR-REM-01 Remote and hybrid work

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains when Contoso Water employees can work from home in 2024 and how hybrid work is arranged. Hybrid work means you split your week between the office and your home.

The policy applies to employees in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands and Germany whose work can be done from a laptop. These are mainly office roles in London, Utrecht and Cologne, such as HR, finance, client onboarding, account management, the service desk and route planning.

The policy does not apply to drivers and warehouse staff. Their work needs them at the warehouse, in the van or at client sites.

This policy starts on 15 January 2024. Priya Shah, HR Manager, owns the policy.

## 2 How many days you can work from home

Under the remote and hybrid work policy HR-REM-01, eligible employees can work from home **up to 2 days a week**.

- The rest of the week you work in your office in London, Utrecht or Cologne.
- The limit is per week, from Monday to Friday. Unused home days cannot be saved for a later week.
- Part-time employees work from home in proportion to their working days. Please agree the details with your manager.

Your manager can ask you to come in on a home day if there is a good business reason, for example a client visit or training. Your manager should tell you as early as possible.

## 3 Company office day

From 2024, Contoso Water has one **company office day**: **Wednesday**. On Wednesday all eligible employees in London, Utrecht and Cologne work in the office.

The company office day is used for team meetings, training and meeting new colleagues. Please do not choose Wednesday as a home day.

If you cannot come in on a Wednesday, for example because of a medical appointment, tell your manager before the day.

Managers may ask their team to come in on other days during busy periods, such as the start of a new client contract.

## 4 How to ask for hybrid work

To start hybrid work under this 2024 policy, you need your manager's agreement and a form for HR.

1. Talk to your manager about which days you would like to work from home.
2. Download the **Hybrid work request form** from the HR page on the intranet.
3. Fill in your name, team, office and your home days.
4. Your manager signs the form.
5. Send the signed form by mail to hr@contoso-water.example.

HR records your home days and confirms by mail within 10 working days. Please do not start working from home on a regular basis before you have the confirmation from HR.

If you want to change your home days later, fill in a new form.

## 5 Working well from home

When you work from home for Contoso Water, the same rules apply as in the office.

- Be available during your normal working hours.
- Answer the phone and mail as you would in the office.
- Work in a quiet place where others cannot see your screen or hear client calls.
- Lock your laptop when you leave it. Do not let anyone else use it.
- Do not print client documents at home.

Contoso Water gives you a laptop. If you need a headset, ask the IT helpdesk at it@contoso-water.example or extension 200. You must have a safe place to work at home, with a proper chair and desk.

## 6 Working from another country

Under this 2024 policy, working from home means working from your home address in the country where you are employed.

Working from another country can cause tax and insurance problems. You may not work from abroad without written approval from Priya Shah, HR Manager. Send your question to hr@contoso-water.example at least 4 weeks before, with the country and the dates.

A holiday abroad is of course fine. Just do not work during your holiday. HR will check the tax rules for your request and reply to you in writing.

## 7 Examples

These examples show how the 2024 remote and hybrid work policy works in practice.

- **An account manager in London** works from home on Monday and Friday. She is in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. This fits the policy.
- **A finance employee in Cologne** asks to work from home on Wednesday and Thursday. Wednesday is the company office day, so his manager asks him to choose another day. He chooses Thursday and Friday instead.
- **A service desk employee in Utrecht** wants to swap her home day from Monday to Tuesday for one week because of a delivery at her house. She asks her manager, who agrees. Because it is only for one week, she does not need a new form.
- **A driver** asks to work from home. Drivers are not covered by this policy, because the job is done in the van and at client sites.
