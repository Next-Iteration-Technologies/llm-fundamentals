# CL-CRD-01 Credit checks

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains when Contoso Water checks a new client's ability to pay, who does the credit check, how long it takes and what each result means.

Contoso Water installs coolers before the first invoice is paid. A credit check lowers the risk that a client cannot pay for the coolers and the water it receives.

The policy applies to new clients in the UK, the Netherlands and Germany. Jonas Becker, Finance Controller in Cologne, owns this policy and does the credit checks. The credit check is step 2 of client onboarding (policy CL-ONB-01).

## 2 When a credit check is needed

A credit check is needed for every new client with an expected spend above £500 per month in the UK, or above €580 per month in the Netherlands and Germany.

The expected spend is the total monthly amount in the signed contract: cooler rental plus any regular extras, such as extra bottles or paper cups. The account manager works out the expected spend from the contract and writes it on the credit check request.

A new client with an expected spend of £500 / €580 per month or less does not need a credit check. The account manager writes "No credit check needed" and the expected spend on the onboarding checklist in Jira project ONB, and the onboarding moves on.

If a new client is close to the limit, or the account manager has doubts about the client, the account manager may ask Finance for a credit check anyway.

## 3 How to request a credit check

The account manager requests the credit check on the day the signed contract arrives. The request goes through the credit check line on the onboarding ticket in Jira project ONB, assigned to Jonas Becker.

The credit check request must include:

- The client's legal company name, exactly as on the contract.
- The company registration number (policy CL-DOC-01).
- The registered company address.
- The expected spend per month, in £ or €.
- The contract length in months.

Finance cannot start the credit check without the company registration number. If it is missing, Finance sends the request back to the account manager on the same day.

## 4 How Finance does the credit check

Finance does the credit check within 3 working days of a complete request. The 3 working days start on the day after the request is complete.

Jonas Becker, or a member of the Finance team he names, looks at:

- A business credit report from an outside credit agency.
- How long the company has been trading.
- Whether the company files its yearly accounts on time.
- Any unpaid court judgments against the company.
- For a returning client, how the client paid Contoso Water in the past.

Finance writes the result and a short reason on the onboarding ticket and in the client record in the CRM. The credit report itself is saved in the Finance folder of the client record. Only Finance can open that folder.

## 5 The three credit check results

Every credit check ends with one of three results.

Pass. The client can go ahead on normal terms. The onboarding moves on to the installation.

Pass with deposit. The client can go ahead, but must first pay a deposit equal to 1 month of the expected spend. The installation is not booked until the deposit has arrived in Contoso Water's bank account. Finance confirms the payment on the onboarding ticket.

Fail. Contoso Water does not install coolers for the client on the signed contract. The account manager calls the client to explain, then confirms in writing. The account manager does not share the credit report or its details with the client; the account manager says only that the credit check was not successful.

A credit check result is valid for 6 months.

## 6 The deposit after a credit check

When the credit check result is Pass with deposit, the account manager asks the client for the deposit on the same day. Finance sends the client a deposit invoice from the finance system.

The deposit amount is 1 month of the expected spend, rounded up to the nearest whole £ or €. For example, a client with an expected spend of £640 per month pays a deposit of £640.

Contoso Water keeps the deposit for as long as the contract runs. The deposit is returned when the contract ends, after the final invoice is paid (policy CL-OFF-01).

After 12 months of on-time payments, the client may ask the account manager to return the deposit early. Jonas Becker decides.

## 7 When the credit check runs late or a client disagrees

If Finance cannot give a result within 3 working days, Finance writes the reason on the onboarding ticket and tells the account manager the new date. A late credit check is one of the most common reasons why client onboarding misses the 10-working-day target in policy CL-ONB-01.

If a client disagrees with a Fail result, the client may send recent accounts or a bank reference to the account manager. Jonas Becker looks at the new information within 3 working days and decides whether to change the result. His decision is final.

A client with a Fail result may ask for a new credit check after 6 months.
