# HR-LEA-UK Annual leave (United Kingdom)

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains annual leave for Contoso Water employees who work in the United Kingdom. It covers how many days of annual leave UK employees get, how to book them, who approves them and what happens to unused days at the end of the year.

The policy applies to everyone employed by Contoso Water Ltd in the UK, including head office staff in London, warehouse staff and drivers. It does not apply to employees in the Netherlands or Germany. They have their own annual leave policies, HR-LEA-NL and HR-LEA-DE.

The leave year at Contoso Water runs from 1 January to 31 December. Priya Shah, HR Manager, owns this policy. Questions go to hr@contoso-water.example.

## 2 How many days UK employees get

Full-time employees in the United Kingdom get **25 days of annual leave** each leave year. UK public holidays (bank holidays) come on top of the 25 days, so you do not use annual leave for them.

UK employees also earn extra days for long service:

- You get **1 extra day for every 2 full years of service** with Contoso Water.
- The total can never go above **30 days** a year, not counting public holidays.
- The extra day is added on 1 January after you complete the 2 full years.

Part-time employees in the UK get the same entitlement worked out in proportion to their hours. For example, someone on a three-day working week gets 3/5 of 25 days, which is 15 days, plus their share of public holidays.

## 3 Examples with numbers (UK)

These examples show how annual leave works for employees in the United Kingdom.

- **Tom, driver, joined 1 March 2021.** On 1 January 2026 he has 4 full years of service. He gets 25 + 2 = 27 days of annual leave in 2026.
- **An account manager with 12 years of service.** 12 years would give 6 extra days, so 31 days. The maximum is 30, so she gets 30 days.
- **A new warehouse operative who starts on 1 July.** For the first leave year you get a share of the 25 days for the months you work. Starting on 1 July means half the year, so 12.5 days, rounded up to 13 days.

If you are not sure how many days you have, open PeopleHub, the HR app. Your remaining annual leave balance is shown on the home screen.

## 4 How to request annual leave in PeopleHub (UK)

UK employees request all annual leave in PeopleHub, the HR app on your laptop or phone. Do not book leave by mail or by a message to your manager.

1. Open PeopleHub and choose **Time off > Request leave**.
2. Pick the type **Annual leave (UK)**, the first and last day, and half days if needed.
3. Add a short note if it helps your manager, for example "school holidays".
4. Press **Submit**. Your manager gets a message straight away.

Give enough notice:

- For 1 or 2 days, ask at least **2 working days** before.
- For 3 days or more, ask at least **2 weeks** before.
- For leave in July or August, ask by **31 March** so routes and cover can be planned.

You will see the answer in PeopleHub. Do not book travel until your leave shows as **Approved**.

## 5 Who approves leave in the UK and by when

Your line manager approves annual leave for UK employees. For drivers and warehouse staff in London, this is Lee Wong, Delivery Operations Manager, or the team lead he names. Managers answer every request within **3 working days**.

Managers look at cover before they approve. In the London warehouse and delivery team, no more than **2 people** from the same team can be on annual leave on the same day, so that every client still gets their weekly delivery. Requests are handled in the order they arrive.

If your request is refused, your manager must give a reason in PeopleHub and suggest other dates. If you think a refusal is unfair, talk to Priya Shah, HR Manager, at hr@contoso-water.example.

A manager can cancel approved leave only in a real emergency and only with HR's agreement. Contoso Water pays any costs you cannot get back.

## 6 Carrying over unused leave (UK)

UK employees should take their annual leave during the leave year, which ends on 31 December. Rest is important, and managers should remind their teams in the autumn.

If you cannot take all your days, you can carry over **up to 5 days** into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March**. Any carried-over days not used by 31 March are lost.

Example: Aisha has 3 days left on 31 December. All 3 days move to the new year and she must use them by 31 March. If she had 8 days left, only 5 would move and 3 would be lost.

There is one exception. If you could not take leave because of long sickness or parental leave, UK law may let you keep more days. Ask HR, who will check your case.

Unused leave is not paid out while you work for Contoso Water. It is only paid when you leave the company.

## 7 Sickness, public holidays and other questions (UK)

If you are ill during annual leave in the UK, tell your manager on the first day you are ill, the same way as for any sick day. The sick days can then be given back to you as annual leave. You may need a doctor's note for this.

If a UK bank holiday falls on a day you normally work, you get the day off and it does not count as annual leave. If you normally do not work that day, part-time staff get their fair share of public holidays added to their balance instead.

Your remaining leave balance, past requests and approved days are always visible in PeopleHub under **Time off**.
