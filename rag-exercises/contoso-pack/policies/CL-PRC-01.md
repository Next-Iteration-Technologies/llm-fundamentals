# CL-PRC-01 Pricing, discounts and approvals

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains how Contoso Water sets prices for clients, who may give a discount, how large the discount may be and how a discount is approved and recorded.

The policy applies to every client contract in the UK, the Netherlands and Germany, for new clients and for existing clients who renew or add coolers.

Jonas Becker, Finance Controller in Cologne, owns this policy. Account managers use it every time they prepare a quote or a contract. Finance uses it to check every contract before it is signed.

## 2 The price list

Every quote starts from the Contoso Water price list. The price list gives a monthly rental for each cooler and a fixed price for each extra. UK prices are in GBP (£). Prices in the Netherlands and Germany are in EUR (€).

Some examples from the price list:

- WC-100 bottle cooler, cold: £18 / €21 per month.
- WC-200 plumbed-in cooler, cold: £29 / €34 per month.
- WC-300 plumbed-in cooler, still and sparkling: £49 / €57 per month.
- WC-400 extra 19-litre bottle: £6.50 / €7.50 per bottle, per delivery.

Bottle coolers include 4 bottles a week. Installation is free on contracts of 12 months or more. On shorter contracts, installation costs £60 / €70 per cooler.

Account managers may not change list prices. Only Finance updates the price list.

## 3 Discount authority levels

A discount is any price below the price list. Who may approve a discount depends on its size:

- Account manager: up to 5%.
- Finance Controller (Jonas Becker): up to 10%.
- Chief Executive (Maria Lopez): above 10%.

No one else may approve a discount. A team lead or the Client Onboarding Lead cannot approve a discount above 5%.

The percentage is the total discount on the monthly bill, compared with the price list. A client who gets 3% off the rental and free paper cups may be above 5% in total. The account manager works out the total and uses that figure to decide who must approve it.

## 4 How to ask for a discount approval

An account manager who wants to offer a discount above 5% asks for approval before telling the client.

The account manager opens a discount request in the CRM on the client's record. The discount request must include:

- The products and quantities.
- The list price and the proposed price, per month.
- The total discount as a percentage.
- The contract length in months.
- The reason for the discount, for example a large number of coolers, a long contract or a competitor's offer.

The CRM sends discount requests up to 10% to Jonas Becker. Requests above 10% go to Jonas first, who adds his view and passes them to Maria Lopez.

Jonas Becker answers discount requests within 2 working days. Requests that go to Maria Lopez can take up to 5 working days.

## 5 Discounts are written in the contract

A discount is only valid when it is written in the signed contract. A discount is never agreed by email, by phone or in a meeting alone.

If a client asks for a discount by email, the account manager answers that any discount will be shown in the contract, and starts a discount request if needed. The account manager does not promise a discount before it is approved.

The contract shows the list price, the discount and the final price for each product. The approval reference from the CRM is written in the contract notes.

If a client claims a discount that is not in the contract, the client pays the contract price. The account manager may start a new discount request, which applies only from the date of a signed contract change.

## 6 What a discount may cover

A discount may cover the monthly cooler rental and the regular extras in the contract, such as extra bottles and paper cups.

Some things are never discounted:

- Repairs caused by damage on purpose or misuse.
- Charges for a cooler that is lost or cannot be returned at the end of a contract.

Installation is already free on contracts of 12 months or more. On shorter contracts, waiving the £60 / €70 installation charge counts as a discount and is added to the total percentage.

A discount lasts for the contract length written in the contract. It does not continue on its own when the contract is renewed. At renewal, the account manager starts a new discount request if the discount should continue.

## 7 Price changes for existing clients

Contoso Water may update its price list once a year, on 1 January. Finance tells account managers about the new prices at least 2 months before.

Existing clients keep their contract price until the end of their current contract length. The account manager writes to each affected client at least 1 month before a new price applies to them.

If an existing client asks for a lower price in the middle of a contract, the same discount authority levels apply. The change must be written in a signed contract change.
