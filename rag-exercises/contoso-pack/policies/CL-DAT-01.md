# CL-DAT-01 Client data: what we keep and for how long

## 1 Purpose

This page explains what information Contoso Water keeps about its clients, why we keep it, how long we keep it and how a client can see or correct it.

It is written for our clients and for Contoso Water staff. It applies to every client in the UK, the Netherlands and Germany.

"Client data" on this page means information about your company and about the people at your company we deal with, such as the person who signed the contract, your billing contact and your site contact.

Sam Patel, Client Onboarding Lead in London, owns this page. Questions can be sent to our service desk at service@contoso-water.example or 0800 123 4567.

## 2 What client data we keep

Contoso Water keeps only the client data we need to deliver water, look after the coolers and send invoices. For each client we keep:

- Company details: company name, company registration number, VAT number and registered address.
- Contacts: names, work email addresses and work phone numbers of the contract signer, the billing contact and the site contact.
- Delivery details: delivery address, access instructions, loading bay, lift and opening hours.
- The signed contract and any contract changes.
- Site survey reports, including photos of the places where coolers stand.
- Delivery records: dates, number of bottles and the name of the person who signed for each delivery.
- Service desk cases and complaints.
- Invoices and payments.
- Credit check results for new clients.

We do not keep home addresses, private phone numbers or any information about your staff that we do not need.

## 3 Why Contoso Water keeps client data

Contoso Water uses client data for four reasons:

- To deliver the service in the contract: installing coolers, delivering bottles, changing filters and repairing coolers.
- To send invoices and collect payment.
- To answer questions, service requests and complaints.
- To meet the law, for example tax rules about keeping invoices.

We do not sell client data. We do not use client data for advertising by other companies.

We may send you news about Contoso Water products, such as a new cooler type. You can ask us to stop at any time by writing to service@contoso-water.example, and we will stop within 5 working days.

## 4 How long Contoso Water keeps client data

Contoso Water keeps client data for as long as the contract runs, plus 90 days after the contract ends.

After those 90 days, we delete the client data from our systems, including contacts, delivery details, site survey photos and service desk cases.

There are two exceptions:

- Invoices are kept for 7 years, because tax law requires it. The invoices show your company name, address and VAT number, and the name of the billing contact.
- If a legal claim is open between Contoso Water and the client when the 90 days end, we keep the client data until the claim is settled. Then we delete it.

If a contact person leaves your company during the contract, tell us and we will remove or replace that person's details within 5 working days.

## 5 Where client data is kept and who can see it

Contoso Water keeps client data in our client system, called the CRM, and in our finance system for invoices. Both are protected by passwords and are only open to staff who need them for their work.

- Account managers and the service desk can see contacts, contracts, service cases and delivery details.
- Drivers see only the delivery address, access instructions and site contact on their route list.
- Only Finance can see credit check results and payment details.

We share client data with outside companies only when they help us provide the service, for example the credit agency that helps us with a credit check. Each of these companies must protect the data and use it only for that task.

## 6 How a client can see or correct its data

Every client may ask what client data Contoso Water holds, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.

To make a request, email service@contoso-water.example or call 0800 123 4567. Please tell us your company name and the request. A person whose own name is in our records, such as a site contact, can make the same request.

- Corrections, such as a new phone number or a new site contact, are made within 5 working days.
- A copy of the client data we hold is sent within 1 month.
- A request to delete data is answered within 1 month. While a contract is running, we need some data to deliver water, so we may only be able to delete part of it.

We confirm every request in writing.

## 7 What Contoso Water does if client data is lost

If client data is lost, sent to the wrong person or seen by someone who should not see it, Contoso Water staff must tell the IT helpdesk at it@contoso-water.example or phone extension 200 on the same day.

The IT helpdesk and the account manager check what happened and which client data was affected. If your company's data was affected, your account manager tells you within 3 working days, explains what happened and what we are doing about it.

Where the law requires it, Contoso Water also reports the problem to the data protection authority in the country concerned.
