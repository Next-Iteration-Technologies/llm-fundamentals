# HR-JOI-01 Joining Contoso Water

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains what happens when someone new joins Contoso Water: before the first day, on the first day, in the first week and during the probation period. It applies to new employees in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands and Germany, and to the managers who welcome them.

Contoso Water rents water coolers to offices and refills them, from offices in London, Utrecht and Cologne. About 120 people work for us. We want every new colleague to feel welcome, know what to do and be safe from the first day.

Priya Shah, HR Manager, owns this policy. Emma de Vries, HR and office manager, looks after new joiners in Utrecht.

## 2 Before your first day

Before a new employee starts at Contoso Water, HR and the manager prepare everything.

**HR sends the new employee**, at least 2 weeks before the start date:

- the signed contract and this joining policy,
- an invitation to PeopleHub, the HR app, to fill in personal details, bank details and an emergency contact,
- the time and address for the first day.

**The manager**, at least 1 week before the start date:

- asks the IT helpdesk for a laptop or company phone and an account,
- names a **buddy**, an experienced colleague who helps in the first weeks,
- plans the first week.

**Drivers** must also upload a photo of their driving licence in PeopleHub before the first day. HR checks the licence with the authorities.

## 3 Your first day

On the first day at Contoso Water:

- **Office staff** start at **09:00** at the reception of their office in London, Utrecht or Cologne.
- **Drivers and warehouse staff** start at **07:30** at the warehouse, so they can see the morning loading.

On the first day you will:

1. Meet your manager and your buddy.
2. Get your ID badge, keys and, for drivers and warehouse staff, safety shoes, gloves and a high-visibility vest.
3. Get your laptop or phone and set your password with help from the IT helpdesk.
4. Take a short tour, including fire exits, first aid kits and the warehouse safety rules.
5. Have lunch with your team. Contoso Water pays for the first-day lunch.

Bring your passport or ID card and, in the UK, proof of your right to work, if HR has not already checked it.

## 4 Your first week

In the first week at Contoso Water, new employees learn the basics of the business and of their job.

Everyone, in the first week:

- reads the key HR policies in PeopleHub, including the code of conduct (HR-CON-01) and health and safety (HR-SAF-01), and confirms with one click,
- completes the short online training on passwords and phishing,
- learns how coolers work: bottle coolers, plumbed-in coolers and the WC-100 to WC-300 range.

**Drivers** also do:

- a half-day of lifting training with Lee Wong, Delivery Operations Manager, or a team lead,
- at least 2 days riding along with an experienced driver before driving a route alone.

**Office staff** spend half a day with the service desk and half a day on a delivery route, to see the work from the client's side.

## 5 Probation period

Every new employee at Contoso Water has a **probation period of 6 months**. Probation is a time for you and Contoso Water to see if the job is a good fit.

During probation there are two formal check-ins:

- **Month 3:** a mid-probation talk with your manager. You talk about what is going well and what needs to improve.
- **Month 6:** the final probation review. Your manager decides whether you pass.

Both talks are recorded in PeopleHub. If you pass, HR sends you a confirmation letter. In rare cases, a manager may extend probation once, by up to 3 months, with a clear plan.

During probation, and during the first 2 years of employment, the notice period is **1 month** on both sides.

Example: a warehouse operative starts on 1 February. His mid-probation talk is in early May and his final review is before 1 August.

## 6 Your buddy

Every new employee at Contoso Water gets a buddy for the first 3 months. A buddy is an experienced colleague from the same team or office, not your manager.

Your buddy:

- shows you around and introduces you to colleagues,
- answers everyday questions, such as where to find things or how to use PeopleHub,
- has a short coffee with you once a week in the first month,
- tells your manager if you need more help.

Being a buddy is voluntary. Buddies get one hour a week of working time for it during the first month. If the match does not work, ask your manager for a different buddy.

## 7 Checklist for managers

Managers at Contoso Water use this checklist for every new employee. The checklist is also in PeopleHub under **Team > New joiner**.

- Contract signed and returned to HR.
- Laptop or phone and account ordered from the IT helpdesk at least 1 week before.
- Buddy chosen and told.
- First week planned, including training and, for drivers, ride-along days.
- Safety equipment ready for drivers and warehouse staff.
- Month 3 and month 6 probation talks booked in the calendar.
- Welcome message sent to the team before the first day.

If something is not ready, tell HR before the first day so the new employee does not wait.
