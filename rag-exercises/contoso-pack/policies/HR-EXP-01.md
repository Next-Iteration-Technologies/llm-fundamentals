# HR-EXP-01 Expenses and business travel

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains how Contoso Water employees claim back money they spend for work, and how business travel is booked. It applies to all employees in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands and Germany.

An expense is money you spend from your own pocket for Contoso Water, for example a train ticket to a client, a hotel night or a meal while travelling. Contoso Water pays these costs back to you if you follow this policy.

Amounts are in pounds (£) for the UK and in euros (€) for the Netherlands and Germany. Priya Shah, HR Manager, owns this policy. Jonas Becker, Finance Controller in Cologne, checks expense claims for Finance.

## 2 The main rules for expenses

These rules apply to every expense claim at Contoso Water:

- **Submit within 30 days.** Claim each expense within 30 days of the day you spent the money. Claims older than 30 days are not paid, unless you were on leave or ill.
- **Always add receipts.** Every expense needs a receipt. A card statement alone is not a receipt.
- **Spend as if it were your own money.** Choose the sensible, lower-cost option.
- **Only real work costs.** Personal costs, such as a film in the hotel or a partner's meal, are never paid.

If you lose a receipt, write a short note in the claim explaining what you bought, where and when. Your manager decides if it can be paid. Losing receipts more than once a year is not accepted.

## 3 How to claim expenses in PeopleHub

You claim all expenses in **PeopleHub**, the HR app.

1. Open PeopleHub and choose **Expenses > New claim**.
2. Take a photo of each receipt with your phone, or upload a scan.
3. Choose the type (travel, hotel, meal, mileage or other), the date and the amount.
4. Write the reason, for example "visit to Tailspin, Cologne".
5. Press **Submit**. Your manager gets a message.

Your manager approves or refuses the claim within **5 working days**. Finance then checks it. Approved claims are paid into your bank account with your next monthly pay, on the 25th.

Keep the paper receipts until the claim is paid, in case Finance asks for them.

## 4 Hotels and meals

When you travel for Contoso Water and need to stay overnight, these limits apply:

- **Hotel:** at most **£150 per night** in the UK, or **€170 per night** in the Netherlands and Germany. This includes breakfast where possible.
- **Meals:** at most **£40 per day** in the UK, or **€45 per day** in the Netherlands and Germany, on travel days. This covers breakfast if not included, lunch, dinner and non-alcoholic drinks.

Meals are only paid on travel days, not for lunch near your own office. Alcohol is not paid, except at a client dinner approved by your manager.

Example: Noor Haddad travels from London to Utrecht for 2 nights to help with the Northwind onboarding. Her hotel costs €160 a night, so €320 is paid. On day 1 she spends €38 on meals and on day 2 €52. She gets €38 and €45, because €45 is the daily maximum.

## 5 Train, flights and taxis

Business travel at Contoso Water should be simple and sensible.

- **Trains:** always book **standard class**. First class is not paid.
- **Flights:** you need **your manager's approval in advance**, before you book. Choose economy class. A flight between London and Utrecht or Cologne is often fine, but check the train first.
- **Taxis:** only when there is no reasonable public transport, late at night, or when carrying heavy equipment. Add the receipt.
- **Hire cars:** only with your manager's approval, and only the smallest suitable car.

Book trains and flights as early as possible, because tickets get more expensive. Keep your boarding pass or ticket with the receipt.

Example: Sam Patel needs to visit Northwind in Utrecht. He asks his manager about a flight, gets approval by mail, books economy and adds the approval to his claim in PeopleHub.

## 6 Using your own car (mileage)

If you use your own car for work, Contoso Water pays a mileage rate:

- **United Kingdom:** **45p per mile**.
- **Netherlands and Germany:** **€0.30 per km**.

The rate covers fuel, wear and insurance, so you cannot also claim fuel. Parking and tolls can be claimed with receipts. Travel from your home to your own office is never paid.

How to claim: in PeopleHub choose **Expenses > Mileage**, enter the start and end address and the distance, and the app works out the amount.

Example: an account manager in London drives 60 miles to a client and back. She claims 60 × 45p = £27.

Drivers do not claim mileage for company vans. Fuel for vans is paid with the company fuel card.

## 7 Things that are not paid

Contoso Water does not pay these costs:

- fines, such as parking or speeding fines, including in company vans,
- first-class train tickets or business-class flights,
- alcohol, except at an approved client dinner,
- personal items, such as clothes, toiletries or hotel films,
- travel between your home and your normal office,
- costs above the hotel and meal limits, unless your manager approved them in writing before the trip.

Gifts and entertainment for clients follow the code of conduct, HR-CON-01, and need your manager's approval.
