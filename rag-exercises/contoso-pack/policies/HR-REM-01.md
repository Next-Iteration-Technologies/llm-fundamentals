# HR-REM-01 Remote and hybrid work

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains when Contoso Water employees can work from home and how hybrid work is arranged. Hybrid work means you split your week between the office and your home.

The policy applies to employees in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands and Germany whose work can be done from a laptop. At Contoso Water these are mainly office roles in London, Utrecht and Cologne: HR, finance, client onboarding, account management, the service desk and route planning.

The policy does not apply to drivers and warehouse staff, because their work needs them at the warehouse, in the van or at client sites.

This version of HR-REM-01 was revised on 1 March 2026 and replaces all earlier versions. Priya Shah, HR Manager, owns the policy.

## 2 How many days you can work from home

Under the remote and hybrid work policy HR-REM-01 (revised 2026-03-01), eligible employees can work from home **up to 3 days a week**.

- The rest of the week you work in your office in London, Utrecht or Cologne.
- The limit is per week, from Monday to Friday. Unused home days cannot be saved for a later week.
- A part-time employee works from home in proportion: for example, someone who works 4 days a week may still work from home up to 3 of those days, but must be in the office at least 1 day.

Your manager can ask you to come in on a home day for a good business reason, for example a client visit, training or a team day. Your manager should give you at least 2 working days' notice when possible.

## 3 Team office days

Each team agrees one fixed **team office day** each week. On that day, everyone in the team works in the office, so that meetings, training and new joiners get face-to-face time.

Examples of team office days at Contoso Water:

- Client onboarding in London (Sam Patel's team): Tuesday.
- Account management in London: Wednesday.
- Finance in Cologne: Thursday.

During big projects, a manager can add a second team day for a short time. In autumn 2026, the Northwind onboarding team in Utrecht may meet in the office more often while coolers are installed across Northwind's 6 floors.

New employees work in the office every day for their first 4 weeks, so they can learn the job and meet colleagues.

## 4 How to arrange your home days in PeopleHub

You arrange hybrid work in **PeopleHub**, the HR app.

1. Open PeopleHub and choose **My work pattern**.
2. Choose your regular home days, for example Monday, Thursday and Friday.
3. Press **Submit**. Your manager gets a message.
4. Your manager approves within **5 working days**, or asks to talk about it.

Your work pattern is then shown in the team calendar. If you want to swap a home day for one week only, agree it with your manager in a short message and update your PeopleHub calendar. You do not need a new request for a one-off swap.

Review your pattern with your manager at least once a year, normally during your performance review in April or October.

## 5 Working well from home

When you work from home for Contoso Water, the same rules apply as in the office.

- Be available during your normal working hours. Keep your status up to date in the chat app.
- Answer calls from clients and colleagues as you would in the office.
- Work in a quiet place where others cannot read your screen or hear client calls.
- Lock your laptop when you step away. Do not let family members use it.
- Never print client documents at home.

Contoso Water gives you a laptop and a headset. You can borrow an extra screen and a keyboard from your office; ask the IT helpdesk at it@contoso-water.example or extension 200. You must have a safe chair and desk at home. Complete the short home workstation check in PeopleHub when you start hybrid work.

## 6 Working from another country

Working from home means working from your home address in the country where you are employed. Working from abroad, even for a short time, can cause tax and insurance problems for you and for Contoso Water.

- You may not work from another country without written approval from Priya Shah, HR Manager.
- Ask at least 4 weeks before, by mail to hr@contoso-water.example, with the country and dates.
- A holiday abroad is fine, of course; just do not work during it.

Example: an account manager in London wants to work for a week from her parents' home in Spain. She must ask HR 4 weeks before. HR checks the tax rules and replies in writing.

## 7 What changed in 2026

The remote and hybrid work policy HR-REM-01 was updated on 1 March 2026. The main changes are:

- **The limit went up.** Eligible employees can now work from home on more days each week than under the previous version of this policy. The current limit is the one in this version: up to 3 days a week.
- **Requests now go through PeopleHub.** Before, employees sent a form to HR by mail. Now your manager approves your work pattern in the PeopleHub app.
- **Team office days** are now agreed per team, instead of one office day for the whole company.
- **New employees** work in the office for their first 4 weeks.

If you agreed a work pattern under the old rules, it stays valid. You may ask your manager for more home days in PeopleHub at any time.
