# CL-OFF-01 Ending a client contract

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains what Contoso Water does when a client contract ends: the client's notice, collecting the coolers, the final invoice, returning a deposit and what happens to the client's data.

The policy applies to every client in the UK, the Netherlands and Germany, whether the client leaves at the end of the contract, leaves early, or moves to another office and stops some coolers.

Sam Patel, Client Onboarding Lead in London, owns this policy. The account manager runs each contract ending, with help from the delivery team and Finance. Every contract ending is tracked on an offboarding ticket in Jira project ONB.

## 2 Notice from the client

A client who wants to end the contract must give notice in writing, by letter or email, at least 1 month before the end date of the contract. Notice given by phone alone is not enough; the account manager asks the client to confirm it in writing.

If a client gives no notice, the contract continues from month to month on the same prices. The client can then end it with 1 month's written notice at any time.

If a client wants to end the contract before the end date in the contract, the client pays the monthly rental up to that end date. The account manager may ask Jonas Becker, Finance Controller, to agree a lower amount, for example when the client moves to a smaller office and keeps some coolers.

## 3 First steps when notice arrives

On the day the client's notice arrives, the account manager:

- Saves the notice in the client record in the CRM.
- Sets the contract end date in the CRM.
- Opens an offboarding ticket in Jira project ONB, using the offboarding checklist template.
- Confirms the end date to the client in writing within 2 working days.

Within 5 working days, the account manager calls the client to ask why the client is leaving. The account manager writes the reason in the CRM. If the reason is a problem Contoso Water can fix, such as late deliveries or price, the account manager tells Sam Patel the same day. Any new price offer follows policy CL-PRC-01.

## 4 Collecting the coolers at contract end

The delivery team collects every cooler within 10 working days after the contract end date. Collection is free of charge.

The delivery team books the collection with the client's site contact, Monday to Friday, between 07:00 and 16:00. On the collection visit, the driver:

- Collects every cooler listed in the contract, checking the cooler numbers against the CRM.
- Collects all full and empty 19-litre bottles.
- For plumbed-in coolers, turns off the water, disconnects the cooler and closes the pipe safely.
- Asks the site contact to sign for the collection.

The last weekly bottle delivery takes place in the last week of the contract. The driver ticks the collection step on the offboarding ticket.

## 5 Missing or damaged coolers at contract end

Sometimes a cooler is missing or damaged when the driver comes to collect it. The driver writes down each missing or damaged cooler, with a photo, on the offboarding ticket.

- A missing cooler: the account manager asks the client to find it within 5 working days. If it is not found, the client is charged for the cooler on the final invoice.
- A cooler damaged on purpose or by misuse: the client is charged the repair cost on the final invoice.
- Normal wear, such as small scratches, is never charged.

If the driver cannot get in to collect the coolers, the delivery team books a new visit. If the client cannot offer a date within 20 working days of the contract end date, the account manager adds Sam Patel to the offboarding ticket.

## 6 The final invoice

Finance sends the final invoice within 10 working days after all coolers are collected. The final invoice shows:

- Rental up to the contract end date.
- Extra bottles and other extras delivered in the last month.
- Any charge for missing or damaged coolers.
- Any amount agreed for ending the contract early.
- Any credit owed to the client, for example for rental already paid beyond the end date.

The account manager checks the final invoice in the CRM before Finance sends it. If the client disagrees with the final invoice, the account manager and Finance answer within 5 working days.

## 7 Returning the client's deposit

Some clients paid a deposit at the start of the contract after a credit check (policy CL-CRD-01). The deposit is equal to 1 month of the expected spend.

Finance returns the deposit within 10 working days after the final invoice is paid in full. The deposit goes back to the bank account the client paid from, unless the client asks in writing for another account.

If the final invoice is not paid within 30 days, Finance may take the unpaid amount from the deposit and return the rest. Finance tells the client in writing before doing so.

Jonas Becker, Finance Controller, decides any question about a deposit. Finance writes "Deposit returned" and the date on the offboarding ticket.

## 8 Client data after the contract ends

After a contract ends, Contoso Water keeps the client's data for 90 days, unless a legal claim is open. Then the data is deleted. Invoices are kept for 7 years. The full rules are in policy CL-DAT-01.

On the contract end date, the account manager:

- Marks the client as "Ended" in the CRM.
- Removes the client's delivery address from all driver route lists.
- Notes on the offboarding ticket whether any legal claim is open.

When the coolers are collected, the final invoice is paid and the deposit is returned, Sam Patel closes the offboarding ticket.
