# HR-REV-01 Performance reviews and pay rises

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains how performance reviews work at Contoso Water and how pay rises are decided. It applies to all employees in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands and Germany, including drivers, warehouse staff and office staff.

At Contoso Water, performance reviews happen **twice a year, in April and in October**. Pay rises are decided after the April review and **take effect on 1 July** each year.

A performance review is a planned talk between you and your manager about your work, your goals and your development. It should never be a surprise: managers give feedback during the year too.

Priya Shah, HR Manager, owns this policy.

## 2 The review calendar

The yearly review calendar at Contoso Water is:

- **1 to 15 March:** HR opens the April review forms in PeopleHub, the HR app.
- **April:** the main review. Your manager looks back at the whole year, sets new goals and gives a rating.
- **May and June:** managers, HR and Finance decide pay rises.
- **By 15 June:** you receive a letter in PeopleHub with any pay rise.
- **1 July:** pay rises take effect. You see the new pay on the July payslip, paid on the 25th.
- **October:** the mid-year review. A shorter check of your goals and development, without a rating.

Employees who are still in their 6-month probation do not have a normal review. They have probation reviews instead, as described in HR-JOI-01.

## 3 How to prepare for your review

Every review at Contoso Water starts with your own view of your work.

1. About 2 weeks before the review, PeopleHub sends you the **self-review form**.
2. Fill it in at least **5 working days** before the meeting. Write what went well, what was hard, and what help you need.
3. Your manager fills in their part of the form.
4. You meet for about one hour, in person if possible. Drivers and warehouse staff meet in the office at the warehouse during working time.
5. After the meeting, your manager writes a short summary in PeopleHub. You read it and press **Agree** or add a comment.

Examples of goals: a driver may aim for fewer than 2 missed deliveries a month; an account manager may aim to hold welcome calls with every new client within 5 working days.

## 4 Ratings

In the April review, your manager gives you one of three ratings:

- **Strong:** you did more than your job asks, consistently.
- **Good:** you did your job well. Most people at Contoso Water get this rating, and it is a good result.
- **Needs improvement:** some important parts of the job were not done well enough.

Before the ratings are final, managers meet with Priya Shah to compare ratings across London, Utrecht and Cologne, so that everyone is treated fairly.

If you get **Needs improvement**, your manager writes a support plan with you, with clear goals and a check-in every month. The October review then looks at your progress.

If you disagree with your rating, add a comment in PeopleHub within 10 working days and ask HR for a talk with Priya Shah.

## 5 How pay rises are decided

Pay rises at Contoso Water take effect on **1 July** each year. They depend on three things:

- your rating in the April review,
- where your pay sits within the salary band for your role,
- the budget for that year, which Maria Lopez, Chief Executive, agrees with Jonas Becker, Finance Controller.

In general, people with a **Strong** rating get a larger rise than people with a **Good** rating. People with **Needs improvement** normally get no rise that year. Someone low in their salary band may get a larger rise than someone near the top.

Salary bands are confidential and are managed by HR. Your manager can tell you roughly where you are in your band.

Pay rises are written in a letter in PeopleHub by 15 June. A pay rise is never agreed by chat or by mail alone.

## 6 Promotions and changes of role

A promotion means moving to a role with more responsibility, for example from driver to team lead, or from service desk to account manager.

- Promotions at Contoso Water are normally decided at the April review and take effect on 1 July, together with pay rises.
- Open roles are shown in PeopleHub under **Jobs**. Anyone can apply, and you do not need your manager's permission, but please tell them.
- If you are promoted, your pay moves into the salary band of the new role.

Example: Aisha, a driver in London, gets a Strong rating in April. In May she applies for the role of warehouse team lead. She is offered the job and starts on 1 July with the pay of the new role.

## 7 The October mid-year review

The October review at Contoso Water is shorter than the April review. It takes about 30 minutes.

In the October mid-year review you and your manager:

- check progress on your goals,
- agree any changes to the goals,
- talk about training you need, for example a forklift course or a client skills course,
- review your work pattern, if you work hybrid.

There is no rating in October and no pay rise. If something important has changed, for example a promotion during the year, your manager will tell you in October what the next steps are.
