# HR-CON-01 Code of conduct

## 1 Purpose

This code of conduct explains how everyone at Contoso Water is expected to behave: with colleagues, with clients, with suppliers and in public. It applies to all employees in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands and Germany, and to temporary staff working for Contoso Water.

The code is short on purpose. It cannot list every situation. When you are not sure, ask yourself three questions:

- Is it legal?
- Would I be happy if my manager or a client saw it?
- Would I be happy to read about it in the newspaper?

If the answer to any question is no, do not do it, and ask HR. Maria Lopez, Chief Executive, supports this code. Priya Shah, HR Manager, owns it. Breaking the code of conduct can lead to a warning or, in serious cases, dismissal.

## 2 Respectful behaviour

At Contoso Water, everyone treats colleagues, clients and suppliers with respect. This applies in the office, in the warehouse, in the van, at client sites, in chat messages and on social media.

We do not accept:

- bullying, shouting or making fun of people,
- harassment, including unwanted sexual comments or touching,
- discrimination because of age, sex, race, religion, disability, sexual orientation or any other personal trait,
- jokes or pictures that offend people.

Drivers are often the only Contoso Water person a client sees. Be polite at reception, follow the client's site rules, and never argue with client staff. If a client is rude or aggressive to you, leave politely and tell Lee Wong, Delivery Operations Manager.

If someone treats you badly at Contoso Water, tell your manager, or Priya Shah in HR at hr@contoso-water.example. If it involves a senior manager, you can go to Maria Lopez, Chief Executive.

## 3 Gifts and hospitality

Clients and suppliers sometimes offer gifts, for example a bottle of wine at Christmas, or invite you to lunch. At Contoso Water the rules on gifts are:

- You may accept a gift worth **up to £50** (UK) or **up to €55** (Netherlands and Germany).
- Any gift worth **more than £50 or €55 must be declared to HR**. It is usually returned with a polite thank-you letter.
- Never accept cash, vouchers or gift cards, whatever the amount.
- Never accept a gift while a contract, price or supplier choice is being decided.

How to declare a gift: open PeopleHub, the HR app, choose **Forms > Gift declaration**, and enter what it is, who gave it and its rough value. HR replies within 5 working days.

Example: a bottle supplier sends Lee Wong a hamper worth about £80. Lee declares it in PeopleHub. HR asks him to return it with a thank-you note.

## 4 Conflicts of interest

A conflict of interest is a situation where your private interest could affect a decision you make for Contoso Water. Examples:

- your partner owns a company that wants to supply bottles or vans,
- you have a second job with a competitor,
- you hire or manage a close relative,
- you own shares in a client or supplier.

Having a conflict of interest is not wrong in itself. Hiding it is. You must declare any possible conflict of interest in PeopleHub under **Forms > Conflict of interest**, as soon as you know about it. HR and your manager then agree what to do, for example that someone else makes the decision.

Second jobs are allowed if they do not compete with Contoso Water and do not affect your rest or working hours. Tell your manager before you start one.

## 5 Company property, information and social media

Contoso Water property includes vans, coolers, bottles, laptops, phones, fuel cards and client information. Use it carefully and only for work, except for reasonable personal use of your phone and laptop.

- Keep client information private. Never share client names, addresses, prices or contracts outside Contoso Water without permission.
- Do not take bottles, cups or other stock home.
- Fuel cards are only for company vans.

On social media, you may say that you work for Contoso Water. Do not post photos of client offices, client staff or delivery notes. Do not speak for Contoso Water in public; send questions from journalists to Maria Lopez, Chief Executive.

Prices and discounts for clients are only agreed in contracts, following the pricing policy for client discounts. Never promise a discount in a chat or mail alone.

## 6 Honesty, bribery and fair competition

Contoso Water wins business by offering good coolers and good service, never by bribes or tricks.

- Never offer or accept a bribe, a secret payment or a favour to win or keep business. This is a crime in the UK, the Netherlands and Germany.
- Never make false records, such as wrong delivery times, made-up working hours or false expense claims.
- Never share prices or plans with competitors.
- Tell the truth to clients, including when something goes wrong, for example a missed delivery.

Small, normal business courtesies, such as a coffee or a working lunch with a client, are fine within the gift limits of this code. Larger client entertainment needs your manager's approval in advance.

## 7 Speaking up

If you see something at Contoso Water that seems wrong, unsafe or against this code, please speak up. Examples: a safety risk in the warehouse, a false expense claim, bullying, or a bribe.

How to speak up:

1. Talk to your manager, if you feel comfortable.
2. Or contact Priya Shah, HR Manager, at hr@contoso-water.example.
3. If the problem involves HR or senior managers, contact Maria Lopez, Chief Executive, directly.

You can ask to stay anonymous. We will look into every report seriously and fairly, normally within 10 working days.

Nobody will be punished for raising a concern honestly, even if it turns out to be wrong. Anyone who punishes or threatens a person for speaking up breaks this code.
