# HR-PAR-01 Parental leave

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains the leave and pay Contoso Water employees get when they have or adopt a child. It covers maternity leave, paternity and partner leave, adoption leave and unpaid parental leave, in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands and Germany.

The basic rule is simple: **Contoso Water follows the national law of the country where you work, and adds a company top-up.** The top-up is:

- **United Kingdom:** enhanced maternity pay at **100% of your normal pay for 16 weeks**.
- **All countries:** **4 weeks of paternity or partner leave, fully paid**.

Priya Shah, HR Manager, owns this policy. Emma de Vries in Utrecht is the local contact in the Netherlands.

## 2 Telling Contoso Water about a pregnancy or adoption

You decide when to tell your manager about a pregnancy. We understand that some people want to wait. Please tell your manager and HR **at least 15 weeks before the expected week of birth**, or as soon as you are matched with a child for adoption.

How to tell Contoso Water:

1. Talk to your manager.
2. Open **PeopleHub**, the HR app, and choose **Time off > Family leave > Tell HR**.
3. Upload the medical certificate or adoption letter.
4. HR contacts you within 5 working days to plan your leave and pay.

For drivers and warehouse staff, telling Contoso Water early is also important for safety. Lifting 19-litre bottles is not safe during pregnancy. Your manager will move you to lighter work, with no loss of pay, as soon as you tell them.

## 3 Maternity leave in the United Kingdom

Employees in the **United Kingdom** can take up to 52 weeks of maternity leave, as UK law allows. The earliest start is 11 weeks before the expected week of birth.

Maternity pay in the UK at Contoso Water:

- **Weeks 1 to 16:** **100% of your normal pay** (company top-up, which includes Statutory Maternity Pay).
- **Weeks 17 to 39:** Statutory Maternity Pay at the rate set by UK law.
- **Weeks 40 to 52:** unpaid.

To get the company top-up, you must have worked for Contoso Water for at least 26 weeks by the 15th week before the expected week of birth. You must also come back to work for at least 3 months after your leave. If you do not come back, Contoso Water may ask you to repay the top-up part.

Example: an account manager in London starts maternity leave on 1 March. She gets full pay until mid-June, then Statutory Maternity Pay.

## 4 Maternity leave in the Netherlands and Germany

**Netherlands:** pregnant employees get pregnancy and maternity leave of at least 16 weeks, as Dutch law sets out. It starts 4 to 6 weeks before the expected birth. The pay comes from the Dutch benefits agency and is paid to you by Contoso Water through your normal payslip, at your normal pay up to the legal maximum. Emma de Vries in Utrecht submits the application for you.

**Germany:** pregnant employees get maternity protection of 6 weeks before the birth and 8 weeks after it, as German law sets out. During this time you receive maternity pay from your health insurer plus a payment from Contoso Water, which together equal your normal net pay.

In both countries you may not do heavy lifting during pregnancy. Drivers and warehouse staff are moved to lighter work with no loss of pay.

## 5 Paternity and partner leave (all countries)

At Contoso Water, the partner of the person giving birth, or the second parent in an adoption, gets **4 weeks of paternity or partner leave, fully paid**. This applies in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands and Germany.

- You can take the 4 weeks at once or in blocks of at least 1 week.
- You must take them within **6 months** of the birth or adoption.
- Tell your manager at least **4 weeks** before you want to start.

The 4 weeks include any paternity or partner leave the law gives you. In the Netherlands, Dutch law gives extra partner leave on top; the extra part is paid by the Dutch benefits agency and Emma de Vries can help you apply.

Example: Lukas, a driver in Cologne, becomes a father on 10 May. He takes 2 weeks right away and 2 weeks in July. He receives his full pay for all 4 weeks.

## 6 Adoption leave and unpaid parental leave

**Adoption leave:** employees who adopt a child get the same leave and pay as for a birth. In the UK, the main adopter gets the same company top-up as maternity pay: 100% of normal pay for 16 weeks. The second adopter gets the 4 weeks of fully paid partner leave.

**Unpaid parental leave:** after the first months, parents can take extra unpaid leave to care for a young child, as the national law allows:

- **United Kingdom:** up to 18 weeks per child before the child turns 18, at most 4 weeks a year.
- **Netherlands:** parental leave as Dutch law allows, partly paid by the Dutch benefits agency in the child's first year.
- **Germany:** parental leave (Elternzeit) of up to 3 years per child, with parental allowance from the state.

Ask your manager at least 8 weeks before, in PeopleHub.

## 7 During your leave and coming back

While you are on maternity, paternity, adoption or parental leave at Contoso Water:

- You keep building up annual leave, as the law of your country requires.
- Your pension and other benefits continue during paid leave.
- You may work up to 10 "keeping in touch" days during UK maternity leave, paid at your normal rate, if you and your manager agree.

When you come back, you return to the same job, or a similar job if the leave was long. Tell your manager your return date at least **8 weeks** before. If you want to change your hours, for example to work part time, ask in PeopleHub; your manager answers within 4 weeks.
