# HR-SAF-01 Health and safety for drivers and warehouse staff

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains how drivers and warehouse staff at Contoso Water stay safe at work. It covers lifting bottles, driving the delivery vans, working in the warehouse, safety equipment and reporting accidents. It applies in the United Kingdom, the Netherlands and Germany, at the London, Utrecht and Cologne warehouses and at every client site.

Most injuries at a water cooler company come from lifting heavy bottles, slips and falls, and road traffic. Almost all of them can be avoided by following simple rules.

Priya Shah, HR Manager, owns this policy. Lee Wong, Delivery Operations Manager, is responsible for safety in the warehouses and on the road. Where national law is stricter, the law applies.

## 2 Lifting 19-litre bottles

A full 19-litre water bottle weighs about **19 kg**. Lifting bottles is the biggest risk for drivers and warehouse staff at Contoso Water.

The lifting rules are:

- Move bottles on a **trolley** whenever you can. Put **at most 2 bottles** on the trolley at a time.
- **Never carry more than one bottle by hand up stairs.** Use the lift if the client has one.
- Bend your knees, keep your back straight and hold the bottle close to your body.
- Do not twist while carrying a bottle. Turn with your feet.
- Lifting a bottle onto a cooler: lift to waist height first, rest it, then turn it over in one smooth movement.

If a client site has no lift and many stairs, tell Lee Wong. He will plan extra time or a second person.

Example: Tom delivers 8 bottles to Fabrikam's 4 floors in London. He uses the lift and a trolley, 2 bottles per trip, which takes 4 trips.

## 3 Driving the delivery vans

Drivers at Contoso Water spend most of the day on the road. The driving rules are:

- **Daily van check** before the first trip: tyres, lights, mirrors, brakes and load straps. Record it in the delivery app on your company phone. Do not drive a van that fails the check; call your team lead.
- **Always wear a seatbelt**, even for short trips.
- **No phone use while driving**, not even hands-free calls. Pull over safely first.
- **Strap down every load.** Loose bottles can roll and injure you in a sudden stop.
- Follow speed limits and park legally. Fines are not paid by Contoso Water.
- Never drive after drinking alcohol or taking drugs, including some medicines that make you sleepy.

Drivers must tell their manager straight away if they get points on their licence or lose it. HR checks every driver's licence once a year.

## 4 Working in the warehouse

The warehouses in London, Utrecht and Cologne store full and empty bottles, coolers, filters and cups. Warehouse staff and drivers at Contoso Water follow these rules:

- Stack full bottles **at most 4 high** on the racks, with the heaviest at the bottom.
- Keep walkways and fire exits clear at all times.
- Clean up spilled water straight away. Wet floors are the main cause of falls in our warehouses.
- Only staff with a valid forklift licence may drive the forklift or pallet truck. Training is arranged by Lee Wong.
- Wear a high-visibility vest in the loading area.
- Visitors must sign in and stay with a member of staff.

Example: in the Cologne warehouse, Lukas sees a leaking bottle on the top rack. He takes it down, puts up a wet-floor sign, dries the floor and reports the damaged bottle in the delivery app.

## 5 Safety equipment

Contoso Water gives every driver and warehouse employee the safety equipment they need, free of charge:

- **safety shoes** with toe caps,
- **work gloves** with good grip,
- a **high-visibility vest**,
- a waterproof jacket for drivers.

You must wear safety shoes and gloves whenever you handle bottles, in the warehouse and at client sites. Wear the vest in the loading area and when you unload on the street.

Worn-out or damaged equipment is replaced free of charge. Ask your team lead, or order it in PeopleHub, the HR app, under **Forms > Safety equipment**. Replacements normally arrive within 5 working days.

Do not wear your own shoes instead of safety shoes, even for a short shift.

## 6 Reporting accidents and near misses

Every accident, injury or near miss at Contoso Water must be reported, however small. A near miss is something that could have hurt someone but did not, for example a bottle that almost fell from a rack.

How to report:

1. If someone is hurt, get first aid or call the emergency number first.
2. Tell your team lead or Lee Wong, Delivery Operations Manager, **the same day**.
3. Fill in the **incident form** in PeopleHub under **Forms > Accident or near miss** within **24 hours**.

Road accidents in a company van: stop, make the place safe, call the police if anyone is hurt, take photos, and call your team lead. Do not admit fault at the scene.

Each office has at least 2 trained first aiders. Their names are on the notice board next to the first aid kit.

## 7 Fitness for work and rest

Driving and lifting are physical work. To stay safe at Contoso Water:

- Take your breaks. A tired driver is a dangerous driver.
- If you have a back problem, an injury, or you are pregnant, tell your manager. You will get lighter work, without loss of pay, until you are fit again.
- If you take medicine that may make you sleepy, ask your doctor if you can drive, and tell your manager.
- Drink water during the day, especially in summer.

Contoso Water offers a free health check for drivers and warehouse staff every 2 years, during working time. HR sends the invitation.

You have the right to stop any task you think is unsafe. Tell your team lead at once. Nobody will be punished for stopping unsafe work.
