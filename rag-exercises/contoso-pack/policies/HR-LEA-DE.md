# HR-LEA-DE Annual leave (Germany)

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains annual leave for Contoso Water employees who work in Germany. It covers how many days of annual leave German employees get, how to book them, who approves them and what happens to unused days at the end of the year.

The policy applies to everyone employed by Contoso Water in Germany, including office staff, warehouse staff and drivers at the Cologne office. It does not apply to employees in the United Kingdom or the Netherlands. They have their own annual leave policies, HR-LEA-UK and HR-LEA-NL.

The leave year at Contoso Water runs from 1 January to 31 December. Priya Shah, HR Manager in London, owns this policy and is the HR contact for employees in Germany. Questions go to hr@contoso-water.example.

## 2 How many days employees in Germany get

Full-time employees in Germany get **28 days of annual leave** each leave year, based on a five-day working week. This is more than the minimum that German law requires.

Public holidays in North Rhine-Westphalia, the state where Cologne is, come on top of the 28 days. You do not use annual leave for them. Carnival days such as Rose Monday are not public holidays, but the Cologne office closes at 12:00 on Rose Monday.

Part-time employees in Germany get annual leave in proportion to the days they work each week. For example, someone on a four-day working week gets 4/5 of 28 days, which is 22.4 days, rounded up to 23 days.

New employees who start during the year get one twelfth of the 28 days for each full month they work in that year.

## 3 Examples with numbers (Germany)

These examples show how annual leave works for employees in Germany.

- **Lukas, driver in Cologne, full time.** He gets 28 days in 2026. He books 2 weeks in August (10 days) and 1 week at Christmas (5 days). He has 13 days left for the rest of the year.
- **A part-time office employee on a three-day working week.** 3/5 of 28 days is 16.8 days, rounded up to 17 days.
- **A warehouse operative who starts on 1 April.** She works 9 full months in her first year, so she gets 9/12 of 28 days, which is 21 days.

Your remaining balance is always shown on the home screen of PeopleHub, the HR app.

## 4 How to request annual leave in PeopleHub (Germany)

Employees in Germany request all annual leave in PeopleHub, the HR app on your laptop or phone. Do not book leave by mail or by a message to your manager.

1. Open PeopleHub and choose **Time off > Request leave**.
2. Pick the type **Annual leave (DE)**, the first and last day, and half days if needed.
3. Add a short note if it helps, for example "school holidays".
4. Press **Submit**. Your manager gets a message straight away.

Give enough notice:

- For 1 or 2 days, ask at least **2 working days** before.
- For 3 days or more, ask at least **2 weeks** before.
- For leave in July or August, ask by **31 March** so routes and cover can be planned.

Do not book travel until your leave shows as **Approved** in PeopleHub.

## 5 Who approves leave in Germany and by when

Your line manager approves annual leave for employees in Germany. For drivers and warehouse staff in Cologne, this is the Cologne warehouse team lead, who reports to Lee Wong, Delivery Operations Manager. Managers answer every request within **3 working days**.

Managers look at cover before they approve. In the Cologne warehouse and delivery team, no more than **2 people** from the same team can be on annual leave on the same day, so that every client still gets their weekly delivery.

German employees should be able to take at least 2 weeks of annual leave in one block each year. Managers should approve one such block for every employee who asks for it in time.

If your request is refused, your manager must give a reason in PeopleHub and suggest other dates. If you think a refusal is unfair, talk to Priya Shah at hr@contoso-water.example.

## 6 Carrying over unused leave (Germany)

Employees in Germany should take all their annual leave during the leave year, which ends on 31 December. In the autumn, managers in Cologne remind their teams how many days they still have, in writing, as German law expects.

Unused annual leave in Germany can be carried over to the next year **only for illness or business reasons**:

- **Illness:** you were ill and could not take the days before 31 December.
- **Business reasons:** your manager asked you to work and moved your leave, and this is noted in PeopleHub.

Carried-over days must be used by **31 March** of the next year. If you simply did not plan your days, they are lost on 31 December.

Example: Lukas has 4 days left in December 2026 because his manager asked him to cover routes. The 4 days move to 2027 and he must take them by 31 March 2027.

## 7 Sickness, public holidays and leaving (Germany)

If you fall ill during annual leave in Germany, tell your manager on the first day you are ill and get a doctor's certificate for those days. Days with a doctor's certificate are given back to you as annual leave.

If a public holiday falls on a day you normally work, you get the day off and it does not use annual leave.

Unused annual leave in Germany is not paid out while you work for Contoso Water. If you leave the company, you should take your remaining days during your notice period. Days you could not take for business reasons are paid with your final salary.
