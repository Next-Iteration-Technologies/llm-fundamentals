# SV-HYG-01 Cooler hygiene, cleaning and filters

## 1 Purpose
This document explains how Contoso Water keeps every cooler clean and safe. It covers the regular sanitisation visits, filter changes on plumbed-in coolers, and what we do when a client makes a taste or smell complaint.

It applies to all drivers, service technicians and service desk staff in London, Utrecht and Cologne. It is an internal document.

The owner of this document is Lee Wong, Delivery Operations Manager, London. The service levels clients see are in SV-SLA-01 Service levels.

## 2 Sanitisation every 3 months
Every Contoso Water cooler is sanitised every 3 months. This applies to bottle coolers, plumbed-in coolers and countertop coolers. The visit is included in the rental price.

During a sanitisation visit, the technician:

- Switches off the cooler and empties the water tanks.
- Cleans the tanks, taps and drip tray with approved food-safe cleaner.
- Rinses everything with clean water.
- Fits a dated hygiene sticker on the back of the cooler.

The service desk books sanitisation visits at least 5 working days ahead and emails the site contact with the date. A visit takes about 20 minutes per cooler.

## 3 Filter changes on plumbed-in coolers
Every plumbed-in cooler has a filter that is changed every 6 months. The filter change is included in the rental price. This applies to WC-200, WC-250 and WC-300 coolers.

The technician usually changes the filter during the same visit as a sanitisation, so the client gets one visit instead of two.

- The new filter is labelled with the date it was fitted.
- The old filter is taken away and not left at the client's site.
- After the change, the technician runs water through the cooler for 2 minutes before it is used.

Extra filter changes outside the included ones are charged as WC-410 at £25 or €29.

## 4 Bottle handling and storage
Clean bottles are part of cooler hygiene. Contoso Water drivers and warehouse staff handle 19-litre bottles in the same way in every office.

- Full bottles are stored in the warehouse out of direct sunlight and away from cleaning products and fuel.
- Bottles stay sealed until they are placed on the cooler.
- A bottle with a broken seal, a crack or a dent is not delivered. It goes back to the supplier.
- Drivers remove the seal cap only at the cooler, and do not touch the neck of the bottle.

Clients are asked to store spare bottles in a cool, dark place and not next to a radiator or a window.

## 5 What clients can do between visits
Clients can help keep their Contoso Water cooler clean between sanitisation visits. Drivers and technicians explain this when a cooler is installed, and the service desk repeats it when asked.

- Wipe the taps and the outside of the cooler with a clean, damp cloth once a week.
- Empty and rinse the drip tray when it is half full.
- Do not touch the inside of the taps or the neck of a bottle.
- Never refill a 19-litre bottle with tap water.

If a client sees mould, dirt or a bad odour, they should stop using the cooler and contact the service desk at service@contoso-water.example or 0800 123 4567.

## 6 Taste or smell complaints
A taste or smell complaint is when a client reports that the water tastes wrong or has an odour. Contoso Water treats every taste or smell complaint as a hygiene matter, even if the cooler was cleaned recently.

When the service desk receives a taste or smell complaint:

1. It records the complaint, with the cooler model, location and the date of the last sanitisation.
2. It tells the client to stop using the cooler until the visit.
3. It books a deep clean and filter change within 2 working days.

The deep clean and filter change for a taste or smell complaint is free of charge. It is not billed as WC-450 or WC-410.

## 7 The deep clean visit
A deep clean is a fuller version of the regular sanitisation. Contoso Water uses it for every taste or smell complaint, and clients can also order one.

During a deep clean, the technician:

- Takes out and cleans every part that touches the water, including taps and the bottle probe.
- Changes the filter on a plumbed-in cooler.
- On a bottle cooler, replaces the bottle on top with a new sealed bottle.
- Checks the water for odour and taste before leaving.

If the taste or smell complaint comes back after a deep clean, the technician swaps the cooler for another of the same model. Deep cleans ordered by the client outside the included ones are charged as WC-450 at £40 or €46.

## 8 Records and follow-up
Every hygiene visit is recorded in the client record. This includes regular sanitisation, filter changes and deep cleans after a taste or smell complaint.

The record shows the date, the cooler model, the technician's first name and what was done. The service desk calls the site contact 5 working days after a deep clean to check that the taste or odour has gone.

Lee Wong reviews all taste or smell complaints every month. Several complaints from the same building or from the same batch of bottles are raised with the warehouse and, if needed, with the bottle supplier.
