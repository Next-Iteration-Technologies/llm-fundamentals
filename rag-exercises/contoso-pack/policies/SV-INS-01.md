# SV-INS-01 Installing and collecting coolers

## 1 Purpose
This document explains how Contoso Water installs coolers at a client's office and how coolers are collected when a contract ends.

It applies to service technicians, drivers and the service desk in London, Utrecht and Cologne. It is an internal document. It covers bottle coolers, plumbed-in coolers and countertop coolers, and also moving a cooler inside a client's office.

The owner of this document is Lee Wong, Delivery Operations Manager, London. The steps before installation, such as the credit check and the site survey, are in CL-ONB-01 Client onboarding.

## 2 Before the installation visit
An installation visit is booked only after the client onboarding steps are complete: signed contract, credit check (CL-CRD-01) and site survey (CL-SIT-01).

Before the visit, the service desk checks that the client record has:

- The delivery address and the site contact's name and phone number.
- Access instructions: loading bay, lift and opening hours.
- The number and models of coolers, and the floor and room for each one.
- For plumbed-in coolers, the water connection points found at the site survey.

The target is to have coolers installed within 10 working days of the signed contract. The service desk books the installation early enough to meet it.

## 3 Installation charges
Installation of a Contoso Water cooler is free on contracts of 12 months or more.

On contracts shorter than 12 months, installation costs £60 in the UK or €70 in the Netherlands and Germany. The charge is per installation visit and appears on the first invoice.

The service desk tells the technician before the visit whether the installation is free or charged, so the technician can answer the client's questions on site. Questions about invoices go to Jonas Becker, Finance Controller.

## 4 Installing a bottle cooler
A bottle cooler installation is usually done by a driver with the first delivery. Bottle coolers are WC-100, WC-150 and the countertop WC-350.

At the site, the driver:

- Places each cooler in the agreed spot, on a level floor, away from direct sunlight and radiators.
- Plugs it in and checks that the socket works.
- Fits the first bottle and lets the water cool for about 30 minutes before use.
- Leaves the first week's bottles, 4 per cooler, in the agreed storage spot.
- Shows the site contact how to change a bottle safely.

Drivers move bottles on a trolley, at most 2 at a time, and never carry more than one by hand up stairs.

## 5 Installing a plumbed-in cooler
A plumbed-in cooler installation is done by a service technician, not a driver. Plumbed-in coolers are WC-200, WC-250 and WC-300.

At the site, the technician:

- Connects the cooler to the water pipe at the point agreed in the site survey.
- Fits a new filter and labels it with the date.
- Runs water through the cooler for 2 minutes and checks every joint for leaks.
- Checks cold water and, where the model has them, hot or sparkling water.

A plumbed-in installation takes about 1 hour per cooler. If the connection point is not ready, the technician does not drill or change the client's pipes, and the visit is rebooked.

## 6 After the installation
When the installation is done, the technician or driver asks the site contact to sign the installation sheet on the delivery app. The sheet lists each cooler, its model and its location.

The service desk then:

- Sets the client's fixed weekly delivery day for bottle coolers.
- Books the first sanitisation, 3 months after installation.
- For plumbed-in coolers, books the first filter change, 6 months after installation.

The welcome call follows within 5 working days, as set out in CL-WEL-01.

## 7 Collecting coolers at the end of a contract
When a contract ends, Contoso Water collects every cooler and all unused bottles. The service desk books the collection for the last week of the contract, or the first working days after it.

- For bottle coolers, a driver collects the cooler, the bottle on top and all spare bottles.
- For plumbed-in coolers, a technician switches off the water, disconnects the cooler, and caps the pipe so it does not leak.

The site contact signs the collection sheet on the delivery app. Any cooler that is missing or badly damaged is noted and reported to Jonas Becker, Finance Controller.

Client data is kept for the contract plus 90 days after it ends, unless a legal claim is open.

## 8 Moving a cooler within the client's office
Clients sometimes ask to move a cooler to another floor or room. Only Contoso Water staff may move a cooler.

- A bottle cooler can be moved by a driver during a regular delivery, at no charge.
- A plumbed-in cooler needs a technician visit and a water connection at the new spot.

The client asks for a move through the service desk at service@contoso-water.example or 0800 123 4567. The service desk updates the cooler's location in the client record after the move.
