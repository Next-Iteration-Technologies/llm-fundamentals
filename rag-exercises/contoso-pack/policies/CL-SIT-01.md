# CL-SIT-01 Site survey before installation

## 1 Purpose

This policy explains the site survey: the visit Contoso Water makes to a new client's office before any cooler is installed. The site survey checks that each cooler can go where the client wants it, and that drivers can get water to it every week.

The site survey is step 3 of client onboarding (policy CL-ONB-01). It applies to every new client in the UK, the Netherlands and Germany. It also applies when an existing client moves office or adds coolers on a new floor.

Sam Patel, Client Onboarding Lead in London, owns this policy. The site surveys themselves are done by the delivery teams, led by Lee Wong, Delivery Operations Manager.

## 2 Who does the site survey and when

The site survey is done by an experienced driver or a warehouse supervisor from the office closest to the client: London, Utrecht or Cologne. This person is called the surveyor.

The account manager asks for a site survey on the onboarding ticket in Jira project ONB on the day the signed contract arrives. The delivery team books the visit with the client's site contact.

The site survey should take place within 4 working days of the signed contract. This leaves enough time to install the coolers within 10 working days, the client onboarding target.

A site survey takes about 30 minutes for a small office and up to 2 hours for a building with several floors. Site surveys take place Monday to Friday, between 07:00 and 16:00.

## 3 What the surveyor checks for plumbed-in coolers

Plumbed-in coolers (WC-200, WC-250 and WC-300) are connected to the office's water pipe. For each plumbed-in cooler, the surveyor checks:

- Water pipe: there is a cold water pipe within 3 metres of the place where the cooler will stand. If the nearest pipe is further than 3 metres away, a plumbed-in cooler cannot go there.
- Drain: the floor and wall near the cooler can take a small drip tray, and there is nothing that would be damaged by a few drops of water.
- Shut-off valve: the client's site contact knows where the water to that pipe can be turned off.

The surveyor writes the distance to the water pipe, in metres, on the survey report for each plumbed-in cooler.

## 4 What the surveyor checks for every cooler

For every cooler, bottle or plumbed-in, the surveyor checks the same three points at the place where it will stand:

- Power socket: there is a working wall socket close enough for the cooler's own cable. Contoso Water does not use extension leads.
- Floor space: the floor is flat and there is enough space for the cooler, with room for people to stand in front of it and room behind it for air.
- Safe place: the cooler is not in a fire exit route, not in direct sunlight and not next to a heater.

If a point fails, the surveyor looks for another place on the same floor with the site contact and writes the new place on the survey report.

## 5 What the surveyor checks for bottle deliveries

Bottle coolers (WC-100, WC-150) need a 19-litre bottle delivery every week. The surveyor walks the route a driver will take, from the van to each cooler, and checks:

- Parking: where the van can stop, and whether a loading bay must be booked.
- Access route: doors, steps, narrow corridors and security gates between the van and each cooler.
- Lift: whether there is a lift that can take a trolley with bottles, and which floors it reaches.
- Storage: where full and empty bottles can be kept on each floor without blocking a corridor.
- Opening hours: when the building is open for deliveries, Monday to Friday.

If there is no lift for bottles and the coolers are above the ground floor, the surveyor talks to the account manager. A plumbed-in cooler or a countertop cooler (WC-350) may be a better choice for those floors.

## 6 The site survey report

The surveyor fills in the site survey report on a tablet during the visit. The report is a form in the onboarding ticket in Jira project ONB.

The site survey report shows, for each cooler:

- The floor and the exact place, with a photo.
- The cooler type agreed with the client.
- The result of each check: pass or fail, with a note.
- For plumbed-in coolers, the distance to the water pipe in metres.

The surveyor also updates the "Delivery" tab of the client record in the CRM with the parking, access, lift and opening hours details. Drivers see this tab on their route list.

The surveyor uploads the report on the same day as the visit. The result of the whole survey is "Ready to install" or "Changes needed".

## 7 When the site survey finds a problem

When the result of the site survey is "Changes needed", the surveyor explains the problem to the client's site contact before leaving the building. The surveyor then writes the problem on the onboarding ticket and adds the account manager.

The account manager calls the client within 1 working day to agree a solution. Common solutions are:

- Moving a cooler to another place on the same floor.
- Changing a plumbed-in cooler to a bottle cooler where there is no water pipe within 3 metres.
- The client asking its own building manager to add a power socket or book the loading bay.

If the change affects the price or the cooler types in the contract, the account manager follows policy CL-PRC-01 and updates the contract before the installation is booked.
