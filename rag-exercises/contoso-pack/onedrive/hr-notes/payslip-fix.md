Note to self: if someone's payslip is wrong and payday is close, don't wait for the monthly PayRight call. Email PayRight by the 20th of the month, cc Jonas, with PAYFIX in the subject line and the employee number. They correct it in the same pay run. Worked for Tom (driver, overtime missing) in July.

(after the 20th they can only fix it next month. then Jonas can do a manual bank transfer for the missing amount, he needs my written ok.)

check afterwards: the corrected payslip shows up in the PayRight app 2 days before payday. if not, call PayRight support.
