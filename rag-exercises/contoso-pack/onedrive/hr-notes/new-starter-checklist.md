My own list for new starters, before day one: contract signed and back, right-to-work check done (UK), bank details into PayRight, PeopleHub account, laptop or phone ordered with IT at least 5 working days before, buddy picked, first week planned with the manager.

drivers: driving licence check with the DVLA code (UK) or a copy of the licence (NL, DE), and the van insurance list updated by Lee. safety shoes ordered in the right size!

after week one: ask the new starter and the buddy how it went. after 3 months: mid-probation chat. after 6 months: end of probation letter.
