Things that help when a manager has a difficult conversation (lateness, attitude, performance): meet in a private room, never in the canteen. Say what happened with dates, not "you always". Ask for their side first. Agree one or two clear actions and a date to check again. Write a short note the same day and send it to the employee.

If it is the second time in 3 months: HR joins. If it is about health: talk about support first, not about absence numbers.
